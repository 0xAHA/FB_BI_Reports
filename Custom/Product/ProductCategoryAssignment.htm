📖 How this screen works

The tree
  • Pick a folder to list its products. ▸ opens it; double-click or ← → open and close; ↑ ↓ move.
  • The number beside a folder counts its products and its subfolders' (a product in two of them counts twice). Active only counts active products.
  • In no folder lists the products in no folder at all; In 2+ folders the products in more than one.
  • Drag a folder onto another to copy it there, with its subfolders and their products (or Copy to… on the folder). Hold a drag over a closed folder to open it.
  • Search finds folders by name (type a : to search whole paths).
  • Drag the tree's edge to make it wider.
Products
  • Filter by number or description (every word must match) and by vendor (a vendor of the product's part); choose the folder alone, with its subfolders, or every product.
  • Tick products to pick them (Shift picks a run). Picks stay when you change folder.
  • Add to folder, or drag the ☰ handle onto a folder in the tree (it drags every picked product).
  • There is no move: Fishbowl's imports only add. To move products, add them to the new folder, then Remove from this folder (a to-do).
  • A folder chip opens that folder; its × puts "remove from it" on the to-do list.
Suggestions
  • Suggest folders looks at the picked products (none picked: the listed products in no folder) and finds similar products already in the tree.
  • Similar means: the number starts the same way (same letters or digits, then a longer shared start) and the description shares words (rare words count more; "and", "for"… don't count). Half each.
  • The ten closest products vote for their folders. The top folder is shown with why ("3 similar: C201, C202…"), and up to two others you can switch to.
  • Strong: most of the votes from two or more close products. Likely: a clear lead. Weak: check it.
  • Products with no good match that share a word are offered as a new folder.
  • Nothing is changed until you accept, and accepting only stages the change.
Applying
  • Every change waits in Changes until you press Apply. It shows exactly what will be imported; nothing is sent until you confirm.
  • Apply sends Fishbowl's own imports: Product Tree Categories (new folders, descriptions) then Product Tree (products into folders). It then reads the tree back and says which changes landed.
  • New folders and descriptions need the Import / Export › Product Tree Categories Import right; adding products needs Import / Export › Product Tree Import. Without them you can still plan, print and copy.
  • A folder name is at most 30 characters, can't contain : and can't be the top folder's name; a description at most 252.
  • Export downloads the folders, and the products in them, in the imports' own format (parents first): a record of the tree, or a start for another company's. It can't undo a change: the imports only add.
Why a to-do list?

Fishbowl's tree imports only add: a product into a folder, a new folder, a folder's description. They can't take a product out of a folder, or rename, move or delete a folder.

So nothing here moves: Add to folder and Copy to add, and taking the old place away goes on the To do in Fishbowl list, with where to do each one. Copy or print it, do them in Fishbowl, and Reload: the list ticks off what Fishbowl no longer holds.

A folder used by pricing rules says so: deleting it changes what those rules cover.
