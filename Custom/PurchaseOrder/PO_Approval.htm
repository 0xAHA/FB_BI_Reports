📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

Every purchase order still waiting on somebody's decision, for the location groups you have access to. That is two statuses, and the Status column says which each order is in:

  • Bid Request — not issued yet, and marked ready: only Bid Requests whose Approval Status custom field is Ready for Approval are listed.
  • Pending Approval — somebody tried to issue it, it was over their limit, and it is now waiting on a higher approver.

Request Amount is the sum of the order's non-void line totals plus tax, in your home currency.

Created By is the user stamped on the PO record. If somebody else has edited the order since, it shows that editor — turn on the Buyer column when you need the buyer instead.

Reviewing and approving

Click Review on a row. The order slides in from the right: who it would be emailed to, then the PO itself — vendor, ship-to, terms, every line and the totals.

At the bottom, Issue (for a Bid Request) or Approve (for Pending Approval) issues it through Fishbowl's own issue path. The server decides the outcome:

  • With enough authority the order becomes Issued and drops off this list.
  • Over your approval limit it becomes Pending Approval for someone above you, and stays here with its badge repainted.

Issue and Email / Approve and Email does the same, then has the Fishbowl server email the order's Purchase Order document to every ticked address. It only emails once the order is actually Issued. Every ticked address, the buyer's included, goes on the To of one email (Fishbowl's email request has no CC). Untick an address to leave it out, pick another of the vendor's contacts from Add a vendor contact, or type one in. The order's note, if it has one, is shown first in the drawer.

The email uses the server's email settings and its standard subject and text. If a send fails, the drawer stays open with the reason and Try again — the order stays issued. Every address that is sent to is recorded in a memo on the PO. Issue / Approve on its own also leaves a memo, saying the order was issued here but not emailed — so whoever emails it later checks first that nobody already has.

The buttons are greyed out unless you hold the Purchase Order → Approve Orders access right.

Filters & columns
  • Status picks Bid Request, Pending Approval or both. Leave it empty for both. Both are always loaded, so switching is instant.
  • Vendor and Created By are multi-selects — tick as many as you like; they filter instantly.
  • The date range re-queries Fishbowl. Pick which date it applies to, then a preset or your own From / To.
  • Click a column heading to sort, drag a heading to reorder, drag its right edge to resize.
  • Columns shows or hides columns, including Status, Buyer, Location Group, Lines, Subtotal, Tax, Currency and Note.
  • Your layout and filters are saved against your Fishbowl user account.
