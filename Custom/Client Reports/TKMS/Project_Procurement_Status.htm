📖 How this report works

What a job needs

A job is a customer. What it needs is every raw-good line on the MOs linked to its sales orders, across all stages. Each part counts once per job, however many lines it's on.

Not counted: sub-assemblies built on the same MO, labour and overhead, and void lines. Requirements shows every line and why it is or isn't counted.

Status of a part (first match wins)
ReceivedHas job PO lines, and every one is fully received. OrderedHas job PO lines, and at least one isn't fully received. In StockNo job PO line, but it's already issued to the build, or on hand in project stock. To Be OrderedNo job PO line and nothing on hand.
Which PO lines count
  • Only lines whose Customer Job is the job.
  • PO Issued, Partial, Fulfilled or Closed Short. Bid Requests don't count until issued.
  • Purchase lines only: not void, credit return, shipping or outsourced lines.
  • A line closed short was neither received in full nor is still coming, so it doesn't count. The part falls back to In Stock or To Be Ordered until it's ordered again.

Job PO Lines lists every line with a Customer Job, including the ones that don't count, and says why.

Tracing a number
  • Click any count on Summary or a tile above the tabs to see the parts behind it.
  • On Part Status, click Qty Required for the MO lines, PO Lines for the PO lines, and a Stock Note for the shared-stock detail.
  • The blue chip above a table says what it's filtered to. Its ✕ clears it.
  • Order, part and PO numbers open the record in Fishbowl.
Quantities

MO and PO lines can each be in their own unit. Totals are converted to the part's stocking unit, which is shown beside each figure. A line with no conversion is left blank and listed on Exceptions.

Quantities never change a status: "Ordered 10 < required 12" is shown as a note only.
