📖 How to Use This Report

Quoting a bar order

Type the product number (or part of the description) and pick it from the list. Then type how many bars the customer asked for.

Every batch in the rack gets its own row, because every batch was cut to its own average length — so the same 5 bars weigh different amounts depending on which batch they come out of. Read the kilos off the batch you intend to supply.

kg for N bars  =  N  ×  Avg Length  ×  Kg per Metre

Hit Copy note on the batch you picked and paste it into the sales order line note. That is what tells the warehouse which batch the quote was based on.

The numbers
  • Kg per metre comes from the Weight per Meter custom field on the part — it is the same for every batch, since it only depends on the diameter and the grade.
  • Avg length is the batch's own tracking value, in metres per bar.
  • Kg on hand is everything in the rack. Kg available takes off what is already committed to a pick and anything in a location that is not counted as available.
  • Bars on hand is worked back out of the kilos, so it is an estimate — bars are cut to an average length and the real bar may be a little over or under. It is shown to one decimal on purpose: 0.8 means there is not a whole bar left, which is why Enough? can say No on a batch that still has weight in it.
If the numbers look wrong

The three inputs are all configurable in Settings → Fields. If a product shows no kilos, or the batch column is empty, the report has not found the right field:

  • no weight per metre → pick the correct part custom field (Weight per Meter);
  • no batch → pick the part's text tracking field;
  • no avg length → pick the part's decimal tracking field.

Stock that was received before tracking was switched on has no batch and no length. It is grouped into a single (no batch) row and cannot be quoted by the bar.
