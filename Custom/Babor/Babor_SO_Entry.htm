❔ How this screen works

What it captures

Every Sale line carries a Pricing Rule. Choosing one sets the line's unit price and writes the rule's name into an order-item custom field, so the companion report can later say what was sold under each rule, and what was given away and why. Fishbowl itself records only the resulting price — several rules can produce the same price for entirely different reasons.

All active rules — everything else, priced locally. Use these to apply a rule by hand (a sample or BDF rule on a retail line, for instance). They are badged off‑list.

The customer note

The note names which rules are in play for this customer, and may name several: PROF 15% & SVC 20% is read as two. Shorthand is matched by its letters (PROF → Professional, SVC → Service), and Settings takes a list of aliases for anything it cannot reach. Numbers must agree exactly — 15% never matches a 20% rule.

Naming a rule does not force it onto a line: as each product is added, every named rule is tested against its own product scope and quantity bracket, and the first that fits is applied.

Limits

Orders are created, never edited — the API has no update endpoint, which is why Estimate is the default status. Kits and drop-ship products are not accepted. Setting a line price needs the SO_PRICE_OVERRIDE right.
