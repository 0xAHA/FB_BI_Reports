❔ How to read this report

The measures

Full price — what the lines would have come to at list price.
Net sales — what was actually charged.
Given away — full price minus net sales. The cost of the discount.
Cost — what the goods cost (see below).
Net profit — net sales minus cost.
Margin — net profit as a share of net sales.

Where cost comes from

Each line shows which basis it used, and you can filter on it.

shipped the cost the shipment actually consumed — the reliable one.

line cost Fishbowl's own figure on the order line.

estimate current average cost × qty, for anything not yet shipped. Treat margin on these as indicative.

Why some lines have no rule

Fishbowl does not record which pricing rule produced a price. Only orders entered through the Sales Order Entry screen carry one, so anything keyed in the desktop client shows as (no rule recorded). That bucket is left visible on purpose — inferring a rule from the price would be wrong exactly where it matters, since several rules give the same price for different reasons.

One caveat on "full price"

Full price uses each product's current list price, because the price at the time of sale was never stored. Changing a price list therefore moves the historical "given away" figures. If that matters, the entry screen needs to capture the list price into a second custom field.
