📖 How this report works

What it's for

One bill often covers goods from several POs: a container's freight, the customs duty on it. Fishbowl's Reconcile wizard can spread a landed cost over one receipt's lines, but not across POs.

This report splits each bill across all the POs' received lines, then tells you what to enter when you reconcile each PO.

Using it
  • Add POs: pick the POs on the shipment. By default only lines received but not yet reconciled are listed.
  • Landed costs: one row per bill. Pick its shipping or service item, enter the amount, and choose how to split it.
  • Tick or untick lines to include them. Service and shipping lines start unticked.
  • Orange cells mean the part has no dimensions or weight. Type them in, and use Save to part to keep them for next time. Blue cells differ from the part record.
  • Dimensions and weight are per one unit of the part's stocking UOM (the /ea beside them), in the unit shown next to them. That unit is what's used here and what's saved. A part with none starts in the unit most of your parts use (Settings can change it).
  • Reconcile entries lays out, PO by PO, the amounts for the wizard's Add Items and Calculate the landed cost screens.
How each split works
  • Size: length × width × height × quantity, in the part's stocking UOM.
  • Weight: the part's weight × quantity, in the part's stocking UOM.
  • Cost: the line's value (billed unit cost, or the PO unit cost until billed) × quantity.
  • Quantity: the quantity received, as Fishbowl counts it (each line in its own UOM).

Each bill is first split between the POs, then each PO's share between its lines, in whole cents that add up exactly. These are Fishbowl's own four methods, so a PO's lines split the same way as the wizard would, give or take a cent of rounding.

Good to know
  • Lines with no dimensions (Size) or weight (Weight) get none of that cost; the yellow notes list them.
  • Foreign-currency POs show amounts in the PO's currency too. The wizard's Amount column is in the PO's currency.
  • Fishbowl only spreads a landed cost over lines with the same Ref No and Bill Date. The wizard's Reconcile step sets both for the lines you tick.
  • Your POs, costs and typed values are kept until you click New.
  • The report never changes a receipt. Save to part only changes the part's dimensions and weight: it reads the part's row from Fishbowl's Part export, sends it back with just those changed, and checks every other field came through untouched. It needs the Part-Edit and Import / Export-Part Import rights.
