Usage Guide

Report Purpose

Monitors inventory for parts with configured reorder points, showing current stock status and recommended order quantities to maintain optimal levels.

Key Metrics

Available: On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Dropship

Qty to Order: the greater of replenishing to Order Up To (Order Up To − Available) and the quantity short of demand — Allocated − (On Hand − Not Available + Dropship), i.e. Fishbowl's "qty needed to fulfil demand". With no reorder point it's simply what you're short. Committed stock is not subtracted again: it is already part of Allocated.

MOQ: Minimum order quantity from the default vendor

Reorder Point: Threshold triggering a reorder alert

Order Up To: Target maximum inventory level

Status Filter

Picks whole parts: a part is listed when any of its location groups has a selected status, and its row then totals every location group, so the breakdown adds up to it. Leave it empty to see every status, No ROP/OUL included. A part with no ROP/OUL in a location group is only listed there when it has stock, demand or orders in it.

Hide Manufactured Parts

Tick it in the filters bar to leave out any part that is the finished good on an active bill of materials — parts you make rather than buy. The setting is remembered.

Columns

Use the Columns button to show/hide columns and drag to reorder them. Your layout is saved automatically to your Fishbowl account.

Export & Print

CSV and Print reproduce the current view — the visible columns in their current order, and the per-location breakdown of any part you have expanded. Collapse a part to export it as a single rolled-up row.

Lead Time

From the Lead Time field on each part's default vendor, in days; — when not configured. It sets the Days of Stock colours; without a lead time the report falls back to fixed 30-day and 60-day thresholds.

Days of Stock

Formula: Available ÷ Daily Consumption Rate

Days +On Order: (Available + On Order) ÷ Daily Consumption Rate — how long you last once open POs land. It assumes incoming stock arrives before you run out; it does not check PO due dates against the run-out date. Hide either column from the Columns button if you only want one.

Daily rate comes from outgoing movements over the selected Consumption Period: shipments (sales and transfer orders) and work-order consumption. A row for one location group — including each row of an expanded part — uses that group's own usage; Company Wide and totalled rows use all usage. Hover a value for the breakdown.

Normal ≥ 2× lead time (or ≥ 60 days)

Watch ≥ lead time but < 2× (or 30–59 days)

Reorder < lead time (or < 30 days)
