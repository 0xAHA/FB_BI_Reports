How this report works

What it does. Finds parts that have fallen below their reorder point at the destination location groups, works out how much each needs, and plans transfers from the source location group — while showing what each transfer does to the source's available stock.

The cap

A transfer is limited to the smaller of two numbers:

  • A percentage of source available (default 35%), and
  • whatever sits above the source's own retention floor (default: the source's own reorder point).

The Cap column tags which of the two bound it — pct or floor. Click any cap to see the full arithmetic. If the source is already at or below its own reorder point the cap is zero and the report will refuse to strip it further.

When several sites want the same part

They share one capped pool. The default policy fills the site furthest below its reorder point first. Change this in Settings ▸ Sharing between destinations.

Typing your own quantities

Editing a quantity pins that row — the engine will never move it again. Remaining sites rebalance around it and any row whose quantity changed as a result is highlighted with a delta chip, so nothing moves silently. Click the pin icon to release a row.

Demand the cap will not cover

The shortfall is written to a deferred ledger and re-offered at the top of your next planning run with its age shown. A deferral is an urgency signal, not extra demand — next run's quantity is always recalculated from live stock, so a site short 100 last week and 80 today needs 80, not 180.

Why the numbers differ from Fishbowl

Fishbowl's available counts intra-warehouse putaway transfers as stock leaving the location group, which can drive the source pool to zero on parts that are in fact well stocked. This report only counts transfers that genuinely cross location groups. Tick Show Fishbowl's own availability column in Settings to see both side by side.

An important limitation

This report cannot stop someone creating a transfer directly in Fishbowl's Transfer Order module — there is no way for a report to intercept that. The Monitor tab catches those after the fact and flags any that exceeded the cap. To make the cap binding, restrict the Transfer Order-Create access right so replenishment is only raised here.
