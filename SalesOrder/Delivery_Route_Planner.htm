📖 How to Use This Report

What it does

Loads the day's shipments (default: packed & shipped today), reads each ship-to address, and builds the best single-vehicle round-trip from your warehouse. Click Optimise Route to (re)plan.

Stops list on the left, route + numbered pins on the map. Click a stop to focus it on the map; the SO link opens the sales order in Fishbowl.

  • Ship status pills (toolbar) — pick which statuses to include. Keep Packed on to pre-build the run while orders are still being packed and marked shipped.
  • Carrier dropdown (toolbar) — pick one, or several to combine multiple drivers' runs into one route.
  • Tick / untick any stop — even before routing — to choose which shipments are included. Excluded stops drop off the map and out of the run.
  • Optimise Route finds the best stop order and draws the road route.
  • Drag the grip handle to set your own priority order (instant, no waiting), then click Plan Route to draw the road route for that exact sequence — it does not re-optimize, so your order is kept.
  • Reverse flips the current order; tick / untick a stop to include/exclude it. Both update instantly — click Plan Route to draw the road route.
  • Optimise Route vs Plan Route: Optimise picks the order for you; Plan keeps the order you set.
  • Round-trip / One-way toggle (top-left) — one-way ends at the last stop instead of returning to the depot.
Exports
  • Google Maps — opens the ordered route for phone GPS
  • QR code — scan to open the route on a phone (also printed on the run-sheet)
  • GPX / KML — for in-vehicle GPS units and Google Earth
  • CSV manifest — the driver's run-sheet
  • Print — a clean printable run-sheet
Setup & limits

Needs internet access from the Fishbowl PC. An admin pastes a free OpenRouteService key under Settings ▸ Admin. Geocoded addresses are cached so repeat runs are fast.

If the map service is unreachable, the report falls back to a nearest-neighbour order and still lets you export CSV / print.
