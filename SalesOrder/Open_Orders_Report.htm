Open Orders Report — what you're looking at

Open sales-order demand, grouped Category → Part → order line, with the part's live availability beside it — so you can see in one pass what is owed to customers and whether stock covers it.

Hover any column heading for its definition and formula. Click a heading to sort, drag it to reorder, drag its right edge to resize.

The three row tiers

The grid reads top-down, and the tiers are not simple totals of each other:

  • Category band — the product-tree node. Counts its parts, totals Qty to Ship, and totals the shortfall clamped at zero per part, so one part's surplus never masks another's shortage.
  • Part row — one per part, carrying its availability. Qty to Ship is the total across the lines currently visible.
  • Order line — one per open SO line. Part-level columns show an em-dash here, because the number lives one tier up.
Qty Required vs Available

These two answer different questions and are meant to disagree.

Qty Required = Qty to Ship − On Hand − In Production. The shortfall against physical and incoming stock, regardless of who it is earmarked for. Not floored, so a negative is the surplus.

Available = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Dropship, floored at zero. What is genuinely free to promise — and since Allocated already includes the demand on these very rows, that demand is netted out of it.

The Location filter narrows stock only

Locations sit under location groups, but Fishbowl's demand-side quantities have no location dimension at all — they are keyed by part and location group.

  • On Hand, Committed, Not Available → the selected locations
  • Allocated, Dropship, On Order → the whole location group, always

So with a Location filter applied, Available reads as "stock in these locations, less everything the whole group owes" — deliberately conservative, and it will not match the unfiltered figure. With no Location filter the numbers agree with Inventory Reorder Watchlist exactly.

Why a part can show Committed with nothing on its lines

Not a bug. The part row counts every commitment against the part, and tag.qtyCommitted includes work- and transfer-order picks — not only the sales orders listed here. Each order line shows only what its own pick holds.

Turn on Show debug console in Settings and the report will split each such part's committed quantity by the order type of the pick holding it.

Scope & filters
  • Which lines appear — sales orders at Issued or In Progress, whose line is not shipped or cancelled and still has quantity outstanding, in the selected location groups.
  • Location Group and Location re-run the queries (availability is scoped by them), so changing either reloads. Product, Customer, SO # and Category filter what is already loaded, instantly.
  • Empty means no filter on every picker — all accessible location groups, all locations, all categories.
  • Category is the product tree, nested as in Fishbowl. Ticking a branch includes everything beneath it. Only branches that actually carry open demand are offered.
  • Product matches the whole part group and keeps or drops it entirely; Customer and SO # are line-level and narrow the lines within a group — which is why Qty to Ship on the part row moves when you use them.
Getting it out

CSV exports one row per order line in the on-screen sequence, with the part-level numbers repeated on each of the part's lines so a pivot has everything on every row — except Committed and Allocated, which export their line-level value, since a CSV row is an order line. Print renders A4 landscape, honouring your column order, widths, sort and filters.
