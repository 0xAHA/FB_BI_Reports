📖 How this works

Finding a report
  • Type in Search (or press /) to match report names and descriptions. Esc clears it.
  • Apps are grouped by folder. Folders are set in the App editor's Apps panel (drag an app onto a folder). Apps without one are under Unfiled.
  • Which modules' Reports › Apps menu lists an app is its module association, shown in the tooltip; it doesn't change the folder.
  • Click a folder chip to show only that folder. Click it again to show everything.
  • Rest the pointer on a tile for a moment to see its full description.
  • In Apps (beta), the ✎ on a tile opens the app in the app editor (with the BI Editor-Edit right; changes also need Edit Settings on the app).
  • Click a section heading to fold it away. Folded sections stay folded next time.
Favourites and recent
  • Click the ☆ on any tile to add it to Favourites. Only you see your favourites.
  • Recent lists the last 8 reports you opened from here.
  • The list shows only the reports your user groups can open.
Opening reports

Open the App Library from the Apps (beta) module (under Reporting). There, clicking a tile opens the app in a new tab, and the App Library stays open in its own. The toolbar's Main Tab and Window move an open app into a main tab or a window of its own.

Anywhere else, clicking a tile copies the report's name and switches to the BI Report module, where you can paste it into the Name search.

Company standard

An administrator picks the reports everyone should start from. They appear at the top for every user.

Admins: click Manage, then use the pin on a tile to add or remove it. Each tile then shows its folder and Apps menus; change them in the App editor's Apps panel.
