📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

Every purchase order still waiting on somebody's decision, for the location groups you have access to. That is two statuses, and the Status column says which each order is in:

  • Bid Request — nobody has acted on it yet.
  • Pending Approval — somebody tried to issue it, it was over their limit, and it is now waiting on a higher approver.

Request Amount is the sum of the order's non-void line totals plus tax, in your home currency.

Created By is the user stamped on the PO record. If somebody else has edited the order since, it shows that editor — turn on the Buyer column when you need the buyer instead.

Approving

Click Approve to issue the order through Fishbowl's own issue path. The server decides the outcome:

  • With enough authority the order becomes Issued.
  • Over your approval limit it becomes Pending Approval for someone above you.

The toast tells you which happened. An order that lands in Pending Approval stays on this list with its badge repainted — it is still waiting on somebody. Only an order that reaches Issued drops off.

Approving a row that is already Pending Approval sends the same call. It clears the order only if your own approval limit covers it; if it does not, Fishbowl leaves the order where it is and the toast says so.

The button is greyed out unless you hold the Purchase Order → Approve Orders access right.

Approving sends immediately. Leave Ask for confirmation before each approval on (Settings → Safety) and every click shows you the order, its status and its amount first.

Filters & columns
  • Status picks Bid Request, Pending Approval or both. Leave it empty for both. Both are always loaded, so switching is instant.
  • Vendor and Created By are multi-selects — tick as many as you like; they filter instantly.
  • The date range re-queries Fishbowl. Pick which date it applies to, then a preset or your own From / To.
  • Click a column heading to sort, drag a heading to reorder, drag its right edge to resize.
  • Columns shows or hides columns, including Status, Buyer, Location Group, Lines, Subtotal, Tax, Currency and Note.
  • Your layout and filters are saved against your Fishbowl user account.
