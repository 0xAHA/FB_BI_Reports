📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

Every purchase order in Bid Request status, in the location groups you can see, one row per line, grouped under its order. Customer Job is the customer the line is bought for.

Pick one or more PO #s or Vendors in the bar above the table (type in the drop-down to search it). Tick Unassigned only to see lines that have no Customer Job yet.

Drag a column header sideways to reorder the columns; the order is remembered. Settings ▸ Reset column order puts it back.

Lines on a Drop Ship PO are shown but locked: Fishbowl will not update a drop-ship purchase order created from a sales order.

Setting a Customer Job
  • Tick lines one at a time, tick an order's box to take all its lines, or tick the box in the header to take every line on screen.
  • Pick a customer in Customer Job in the bar (type to search) and click Apply to selected. Pick None (clear) to clear the ticked lines.
  • Or pick one straight from a line's own Customer Job box.
  • Changed lines turn orange. Nothing is written until you click Save changes. Discard undoes them.
How saving works

Each changed order is sent back through Fishbowl's Purchase Order import. The whole order goes back: header, every line and every custom field, so the import keeps what was already there. The order is then re-read and checked, and a report lists anything that did not come back as expected.

An order that changed in Fishbowl after you loaded it, or has left Bid Request, is skipped. Refresh and try again.

Two customers with the same name can't be told apart by the import, so the report won't assign either of them.
