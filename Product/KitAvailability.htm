📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

One row per kit product × location group. Kits Available is how many complete kits you could assemble and sell right now from stock in that location group, as the kit is configured — capped by the scarcest kit line (the constraint part).

"Available" means Available for Sale = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Drop Ship, the same basis as Fishbowl's Inventory Availability and Available to Build reports.

The three figures

Kits Available is the kit exactly as it is configured — every line, each at its default option choice. It is the conservative figure and the one that matches Fishbowl's own Kit Availability report.

The other two are the ways of beating it. Excl. Optional drops the Optional and Variable-Optional lines, which an order does not include unless the salesperson picks them — when it is higher, an optional line is what is holding the kit back. Best Variation keeps every line but takes the best-stocked choice on each.

Discount and Tax Rate lines are never components and are always ignored.

Variations

An optional or option-group line lets the salesperson pick from several products, so the kit has variations. Best Variation is how many you could make with the best-stocked choice on every option line — when it beats Kits Available, the kit isn't really blocked, it just needs a different option picked, and the expanded panel names which one.

Inside the panel each option shows its own stock and the kit total it would produce. These totals are alternatives, not additions — two variations of the same kit draw on the same shared components, so you cannot make the best of both.

Drill & columns
  • Click any row to expand its kit lines underneath: each line's available stock and how many kits it supports, with the limiting line(s) highlighted. Click again to collapse. Expand all in the header opens every row at once.
  • Qty / Kit is shown in the part's stocking UOM; where the kit line was authored in another unit, the original is in brackets.
  • An option line expands (▸) to list every selectable alternative with its own availability — useful when the default choice is the bottleneck.
  • After Incoming projects the count once open POs/MOs/TOs land.
  • Sort by clicking a header; reorder by dragging headers; resize by dragging a header edge. Your layout is saved.
Filters & export

By default only kits you can build now are listed — switch to All kits or Zero-available in the filter drawer, where you can also multi-select Location Group, Kit Product and Constraint Part, and search a kit number. CSV and Excel export exactly the rows on screen; Print opens a print-formatted window.
