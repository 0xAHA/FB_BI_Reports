📖 How to read this report

What you're looking at

One row per Manufacture Order (blue header) and one indented row per Work Order beneath it. Click an MO chevron to fold its WOs; click a WO chevron to drill into the raw-material lines being consumed.

Progress %
  • WO row: step-count from woinstruction — completed steps (qtyFulfilled ≥ qtyReady) ÷ total steps.
  • MO row: qty-weighted average of its WOs by wo.qtyTarget.
Pick column

Aggregate of every pick attached via pickitem.woItemId. Badge shows the worst still-open status; the count is finished/total. "No picks" means none have been generated yet.

WIP value KPI

Sum of tag.qty × partcost.avgCost for tags bound to in-progress WOs via tag.woItemId. Uses avg cost — close approximation for FIFO/LIFO but not layer-accurate.

Open requirements KPI

Value of raw-material woitem lines (typeId=20) not yet fulfilled, at avg cost — money that still needs to flow into WIP.

Cost column

Running cost of the WO = parts staged in WIP (tag.qty × avgCost) + parts already consumed (woitem.qtyUsed × avgCost). For a Fulfilled WO (status 40) the WIP portion settles to 0 and this becomes the actual cost. The MO row sums across its WOs. Hover for the staged vs. consumed split.

MO/WO data model

The output part is read through moitem(typeid=50, parent) → child moitem(typeid=10) → part. wo.moItemId points at the typeid=50 parent. The MO's "finished good" column shows typeid=10 outputs with stagelevel=1 (top of the stage tree).
