📖 Instructions

About

Two interactive views for visualizing and managing WO schedules:

  • MO Gantt View: Groups work orders by Manufacturing Order
  • Capacity Planning: Weekly calendar with drag-and-drop scheduling and capacity tracking
Gantt View
  • Drag WO: Reschedule by dragging the bar
  • Resize: Drag the left/right edge to change start/finish
  • Click a WO: Opens the detail drawer — set exact start/finish (with time) and category there
  • Navigate: Scroll timeline, prev/next week buttons
  • Zoom: Toolbar zoom controls (persists across sessions)
  • Collapse MOs: Chevron on the MO row folds it; "All" in the column header folds every group
  • Undo/Redo: Toolbar buttons (50-step history)
Capacity Planning
  • Weekly: Drag horizontally (dates) or vertically (categories)
  • Monthly: Drag WOs across days; nav with month buttons or Today
  • Categories: Sorted alphabetically
  • Capacity limits: Gear icon inside the capacity view sets daily labor hours per category (auto-saves)
  • Color coding: Green = under, Orange = at, Red = over capacity
Settings
  • Auto-save: Every toggle change writes to your Fishbowl user account via saveSettings — no fake-part rows anymore.
  • First load migration: If you had settings on the older report, they'll be read from the legacy _settingsCapacityPlanner part once and carried forward automatically.
