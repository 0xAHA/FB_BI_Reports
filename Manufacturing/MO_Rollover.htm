📖 How MO Rollover works

The list

Issued and Partial MOs in your location groups. Scheduled narrows it by the MO's scheduled date: pick a preset, counted from today each time the report opens, or type your own From / To, which switches the range to Custom.

Which MOs can roll over

Only a Partial MO — at least one WO already fulfilled. Fishbowl won't close an Issued MO short, and with nothing built there is nothing to close it short for; those show as Nothing built yet.

One exception: an Issued MO whose open WOs already went to a new MO in an earlier rollover. Those WOs are duplicates, so the report offers to unissue and delete it — always after asking you first.

What happens to each WO
  • Moves — Entered, nothing picked, used or scrapped, no WIP stock. It is rebuilt on the new MO, and closing the original short removes it together with its pick.
  • Stays — Fulfilled. It stays on the original, which is closed short.
  • Blocks — anything else. Fishbowl won't close an MO short while one of its WOs is started or its pick is in progress:
    • a picked WO (pick lines started, committed or finished — for example a finished sub-stage's output committed to the next stage): void its pick in the Picking module, then press Re-check
    • a started WO, stock in WIP or quantities already used: finish the WO in Fishbowl

Every movable WO moves — there is no opt-out, because a WO left behind would be closed short with the original.

What the button does
  • Creates the new MO as Entered and checks every line against what was asked for
  • Closes the original short (/close-short)
  • Issues the new MO, which generates its work orders
  • Copies each old WO's location, customer, calendar category, assigned users, note and dates to its new WO

Nothing is deleted. If a step fails the run stops there and tells you exactly what was left behind. An Entered MO left by a run that stopped is used again by the next run, as long as it still matches what is left to build. If that MO has since been issued, the work is already there and the run only closes the original short.

How the new MO is built
  • Numbered after the original: 117 → 117-1, then 117-2 …
  • One build per moving WO, with that WO's current lines and quantities, dates, priority and class
  • A staged build whose WOs all move is rebuilt from its BOM so the stages are kept — but only when the BOM gives exactly the same lines as the WOs. Otherwise each WO becomes its own build and the sub-assembly link is not kept.
  • Not carried: the sales-order link, MO memos, WO custom fields and WO instruction edits
