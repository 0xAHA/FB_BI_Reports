📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

One row per BOM × location group. Available to Build is how many complete finished units you could build right now from on-hand stock — capped by the scarcest component (the constraint part).

"Available" here means Available for Sale = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Drop Ship, matching Fishbowl's standard Available to Build report.

Drill & columns
  • Click the Components cell to see every component, its available stock, and how many builds each supports — the limiting one(s) are highlighted.
  • A staged sub-assembly expands to show its own components and build count.
  • Build After Incoming projects the build count once open POs/MOs arrive.
  • Sort a column by clicking its header; reorder by dragging headers; resize by dragging a header edge. Your layout is saved.
  • Click a BOM # or Finished Good to open it in Fishbowl; click a Constraint or Components cell for the full component breakdown.
Filters & settings

By default only BOMs you can build now are listed — switch to All BOMs or Zero-buildable in the filter drawer. There you can also multi-select Location Group, Finished Good and Constraint Part (e.g. "show every BOM blocked by part X"), and search a BOM number. Settings (cost basis, debug) persist to your Fishbowl user account; admins can Publish defaults for everyone.
