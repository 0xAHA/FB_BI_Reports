📖 How this tool works

What it does

Counts one part's stock location by location and posts each counted location to Fishbowl as a cycle count. Untracked inventory parts only: a tracked part needs its tracking on every count line.

Counting
  • Pick the part: type part of its number or description, or press ▾ to browse.
  • Each location holding it is a line. Type the counted quantity in New count; leave the lines you didn't count empty. Enter or the arrow keys move between lines; Esc clears a count.
  • Locations limits the lines to the locations you are counting. Add a location counts one that holds none of the part yet; × removes a line.
  • A count can't go below the quantity committed to orders there.
Posting
  • Fill in Details (the reason and who counted are required).
  • Post count reads the stock again, lists every change for you to confirm, then posts one location at a time. Stop stops after the location being posted.
  • Afterwards the stock is read back: Posted means Fishbowl holds the count; Check means it holds something else.
  • Print prints a count sheet, or after posting, the record of what was posted.
  • Posting needs the Inventory › Cycle right.
