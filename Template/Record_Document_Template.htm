📖 How this report works

What it shows

One transfer order, as the standard Transfer Order Report prints it: the location group it comes from and the one it goes to, their addresses (Ship transfers), the note, the owner, type, status, carrier and dates, and every item to transfer with its quantity and UOM.

The company address in the header is the address of the owner location group. The number is printed with a "T" in front when the company's barcodes carry the order type.

Choosing the transfer order
  • Open Filters and type a TO number, a location group or a part number: the list shows up to 20 matching transfer orders with their from / to groups, status, type and date. The status and type chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next one.
  • The list covers the transfer orders from or to your location groups; type an exact number and press Enter to open any other.
  • The report opens on the transfer order you looked at last (else the newest open one). When the BI report's Transfer_Order setting names a transfer order, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a transfer order (the Transfer Order module's Apps menu), the report shows that transfer order and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (barcode, logo position, company name and address, country in the addresses, revision in the footer), with the standard report's saved options as the starting point. Page layout prints on landscape (or Legal landscape) paper. Changes apply straight away.
  • Progress columns (Settings) add each item's status, picked and fulfilled quantities and dates to this view and to Excel.
  • The transfer order's custom fields show on the card; Custom fields on the printed page prints them too.
  • Click the transfer order number or a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Columns (above the items) shows, hides and orders the item columns; admins click a column's name to rename it for everyone. Click a heading to sort the items, drag a heading to move it, drag its right edge to resize it. The printed item table follows your columns and names; printed from a module (Print / Email), it prints the standard columns with the company's names.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the items with their notes.
