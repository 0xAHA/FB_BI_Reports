📖 How this report works

What it shows

TEMPLATE: what the report shows. (The example: how old the inventory on hand is, every tag of stock with when it was created and how many days ago that was.)

Tags of the same part, in the same location, with the same tracking (lot, expiry…) are added together into one row. Created is the oldest of those tags; Days is the whole days since then; Age is its age band.

Filters and views
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Created picks a quick range or your own dates.
  • The bookmark button saves the filters and the Table / Pivot choice as a view. Star one to open it by default. Admins also publish Company views for everyone.
  • Settings → Remember my filters reopens the report on your last filters.
Using it
  • The tiles above the table sum up what the filters return.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its tags; Expand / Collapse do every group. Click a tag row to see its details; click a part number to open the part.
  • Columns shows, hides and orders the columns, the part's custom fields included. Admins click a column's name to rename it for everyone. Excel and the printed pages use the same columns and names.
  • Click a column heading to sort the tags within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty, Days or the number of tag sets by any two of location group, location, part, UOM, age band and Created. Click a figure to see the tags behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and the printable pages; in PDF view pick the paper in the bar above the pages. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so tag sets (whole groups) with a Load all button.
