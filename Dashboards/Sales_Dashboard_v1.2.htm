📖 Instructions

Layout
  • Click the padlock to unlock editing — drag a widget by its header, resize from any edge
  • Add Widget opens the picker; Save Layout stores your personal arrangement
  • KPI badges in the header: drag to reorder, hover for ✕, + to add more
Filters & data
  • The filter strip applies to every widget — date range plus customer group, customer, category, salesperson and margin tier (the margin tier keeps order lines whose own margin is in that tier)
  • Compare (filter strip, or Off / Prev / LY on the trend tile) adds the previous period or the same period last year to the KPIs and badges, the trend chart (comparison bars sit beside each bar), the revenue tables, the Top Customers / Sales People / Products bars, and powers the Profit Bridge and Growth vs Margin tiles (Add Widget). It is off by default and always loads after everything else
  • Margins follow the Fishbowl Margins report: (revenue − cost) ÷ revenue over all lines in the group, credits included; shipping, tax, subtotal and note lines are left out
  • Click a Top Customers bar or an order number to drill into the underlying orders
  • Categories show their full Product Tree path — set the roll-up depth in Settings ▸ Product categories ▸ Category level; admins publish it as the org default with Publish
  • Admins (Fishbowl Admin access) can Publish the current layout/filters as the org-wide default and lock user editing
