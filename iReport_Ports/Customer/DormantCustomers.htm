📖 How this report works

What it shows

Customers who have not ordered for at least the dormant period, but whose last order is no older than the last-order limit. With the defaults: no order in the last 90 days, last order within 365 days. It is the BI version of the standard Dormant Customers report and reads the same data the same way.

Which orders count
  • Sales orders that are Estimate, Issued, In Progress, Fulfilled or Closed Short. Voided, Cancelled and Expired orders never count.
  • The last order is the latest Date Issued by default; pick Date Created, Date Scheduled or Date Completed instead. Orders without that date are ignored, so on Date Completed an open order doesn't make a customer active.
  • Total Orders and Total Spent cover every counted order, of any date. Estimates are included, as in the standard report.
Columns
  • Sales Rep is the customer's default salesperson; State is from its default Main Office address.
  • Last SO# is the order on the last date. If two share that date, the most recently created one is shown.
  • A customer custom field can be shown as an extra column, and the list filtered on any field (% is a wildcard; for a checkbox field type true or false).
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Sorting and the column switches don't re-run the query.
  • Click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse, a third time for the standard order (Days Dormant, ascending). It sets Sort by and Order in the filters. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: count the dormant customers, or total what they spent, by any two of sales rep, state, active / inactive, the month of their last order and the custom field. Click a figure to see the customers behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result shows the first 10,000 customers in the chosen sort, with a Load all button; totals then cover the customers shown.
  • Click a customer or SO number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
