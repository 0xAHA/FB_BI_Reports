📖 How this report works

What it shows

A statement for each customer: their sales orders that still have a balance, the payments taken on each, and what is left to pay. It is the BI version of the standard Customer Statement and reads the same data the same way.

Payments are the ones taken on the sales order in Fishbowl. Payments recorded only in your accounting system are not here.

Date due
  • Net terms (and COD, CIA and the like): the base date plus the net days.
  • Month terms: the net days are a day of the month. If that day has passed, it is next month's; if it then falls within the term's "next month" days of the base date, the month after.
  • Days after EOM terms: the end of the base date's month plus the days.
  • The base date is the one in Add net days to. With Due date includes net days off, the due date is the order's Date Scheduled.
Totals
  • An order's Balance is its total less the payments listed under it.
  • Orders paid in full are left out unless Orders with a 0 balance is on.
  • With the range on Date Paid, only payments in the range are listed and counted, and orders with no payment in it drop out.
  • An old order saved before Fishbowl stored order totals shows its lines' total plus tax.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a customer's row to show or hide their orders; Expand / Collapse do every customer. Print follows what is shown.
  • Click a customer or order number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click an order column's heading (SO, Terms, Date Due, Total Paid, Order Total, Balance) to sort each customer's orders by it (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Payments stay in date order under their order. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot of the orders: total the Balance (or Order Total, Paid, payments) by any two of customer, payment terms, due date (by month, quarter…) and how overdue the order is. Click a figure to see the orders behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result shows the first customers that fit in 10,000 rows, each one complete, with a Load all button; totals then cover the customers shown.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
