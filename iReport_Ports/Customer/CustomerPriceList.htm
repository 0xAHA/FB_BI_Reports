📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every active product with the price one customer pays for it. It is the BI version of the standard Customer Price List and works the price out the same way: one unit, today, after all of Fishbowl's pricing rules that reach this customer (their own, their customer groups', and everyone's).

The prices
  • Price: what a sales order would charge this customer for one unit today. Hover it to see the pricing rule that set it.
  • Org. Price: the list price before customer rules, which is the product's own price unless a Tier 2 rule changes it.
  • Include tax in price adds the product's tax rate to Price only.
  • A price marked list is the product's own price: this report couldn't work out the customer's price for it. The banner says why.
Using it
  • Pick a customer first; nothing loads until you do. Other filter changes apply straight away.
  • A product in two categories is listed under each. Click a category's row to show or hide it; Expand / Collapse do them all.
  • Text searches match anywhere in the value. Type a % to write your own pattern (ABC% = starts with ABC).
  • Click a column heading to sort the products within each category (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Price, Org. Price or Discount (Org. Price − Price, before tax), or a count of products, by any two of product, product tree, price basis (the pricing rule that set the price) and tax code. Click a figure to see the products behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large list shows its first 10,000 lines with a Load all button.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
