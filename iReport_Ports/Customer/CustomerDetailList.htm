📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every customer's addresses, and the contacts saved on each address. It is the BI version of the standard Customer Detail List and reads the same data the same way.

A customer with no address of the chosen type is left out, and so are its jobs. Contacts that aren't attached to an address aren't listed.

Jobs

With Include jobs on, each customer's jobs follow it as Customer:Job, indented, and a job's own jobs follow that. The active / inactive filter applies to the customers you list, not to their jobs, as in the standard report.

With no customer chosen, the list starts from top-level customers. Choose a job to list it on its own.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a customer's row to show or hide its addresses and jobs; Expand / Collapse do every customer. Click the name to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort each customer's addresses (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); customers stay in name order and contacts stay under their address. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • A very long list shows its first 2,500 customers, each complete with its addresses, contacts and jobs, and a Load all button.
  • Excel saves one row per contact (or per address, with contact info off or no contacts), with the customer and its parent.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
