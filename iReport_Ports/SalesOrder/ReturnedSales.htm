📖 How this report works

What it shows

The inventory items customers returned: every Credit Return line with a fulfilled quantity, on sales orders completed in the date range, by product, customer and salesperson. Only products of Inventory parts count. It is the BI version of the standard Returned Sales report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Qty Fulfilled is the quantity received back, in the line's UOM.
  • Price is the credit's total (negative, as Fishbowl stores a credit return), plus the line's adjustment per unit, as the standard report adds it. Amounts are in the home currency.
  • A product's row carries its total price. Two rows can look the same when the orders were entered by different users; hover the customer to see who.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Product number takes % as a wildcard.
  • Click a product's row to show or hide its rows; Expand / Collapse do every product.
  • Click a column heading to sort the rows within each product; drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot of the price, qty or row count by any two of product, customer, salesperson and UOM. Click a figure to see the rows behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
