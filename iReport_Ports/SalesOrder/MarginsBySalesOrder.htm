📖 How this report works

What it shows

Each sales order's price, cost of goods sold (COGS) and margin, optionally with its lines. It is the BI version of the standard Margins By Sales Order report and reads the same data the same way.

Where the figures come from

Shipped quantities: the price on the line (with a positive adjustment spread over it) and the cost Fishbowl posted when it shipped, dated when it was posted.

Unshipped quantities (Estimate, Issued, In Progress and Voided orders): the line's price and the part's average cost now. Show only shipped items leaves them out.

Charges & credits: misc. credits, discounts and shipping, at their posted (or credited) price and the line's cost.

Margin % is the margin over the price, shown as 0 when the margin is negative or the price is 0, as the standard report does.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Choosing no location groups, customers or salespeople means all of them; untick a status to leave its orders out.
  • Show details lists each order's lines under it; click an order's row to hide or show them, or use Expand / Collapse. More columns adds each line's date and where its figures come from.
  • COGS needs the Sales Order-Cost right and Margins / Margin % the Sales Order-Margin right (or admin); without them those columns are left out everywhere.
  • Click a column heading to sort the orders (or, with details, the lines within each order): again to reverse, a third time for the standard order. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: price, COGS, margins, qty or margin % (total margin over total price) by any two of customer, salesperson, location group, status, product, basis and date. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so rows (whole orders) with a Load all button; the totals then cover the orders shown.
