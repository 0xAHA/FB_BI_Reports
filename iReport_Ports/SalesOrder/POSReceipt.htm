📖 How this report works

What it shows

The point-of-sale receipt for one sales order: the BI version of the standard POS Receipt (a Letter page), Receipt 3in and Receipt 2in (receipt-printer rolls), reading the same data the same way.

  • The App view is the order on screen: its lines, totals and payments.
  • The PDF view is the printed receipt. A roll receipt is one long page at the roll's width, as tall as the receipt.
Choosing the order and format
  • Open Filters and type an SO number, a customer or a customer PO. The arrows step to the previous / next order.
  • Receipt › Format picks Letter, 3 in roll or 2 in roll. It is remembered like your other options, and an admin can make one everyone's default (Settings › Admin), which is also the format the receipt prints in when another program asks for it.
  • Opened from a sales order (report_btn's Apps menu), the report shows that order and the picker is hidden.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (and the Letter receipt's line columns in Settings); only the chosen format's options show. The standard report's saved options are the starting point; Reset options goes back to them.
  • Gift receipt (rolls) leaves out the prices, payments and totals.
  • Print prints the receipt: the Letter pages, or the roll as one page of its own size.
  • Excel saves the lines and the payments.
