📖 How this report works

What it shows

How many Point of Sale orders were completed in each hour of the working day, day by day, with each hour's share of the day's orders, and a chart of the average sales per hour over the range. It is the BI version of the standard POS Hourly Breakdown report and counts the same orders the same way.

The figures
  • An order counts in the hour it was completed. Only Point of Sale orders count.
  • Only the ticked days of the week (Monday to Friday by default) and the hours of the working day (7 to 19 by default, 19 meaning up to 19:59) count; a day with no orders in them is left out.
  • % of Daily Sales is the hour's orders over the day's, rounded to a whole percent.
  • The chart's average for an hour is its orders over the range divided by the number of days listed.
  • "All salespeople" leaves out orders with no salesperson, as the standard report does.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a day's row to show or hide its hours; Expand / Collapse do every day.
  • Click a column heading to sort the hours within each day (a third click for the hour order); drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: orders by any two of weekday, hour and day (or week, month…). Hour × Weekday shows the busy hours of each day of the week.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report: the days, then the chart on its own page. Print prints those pages.
