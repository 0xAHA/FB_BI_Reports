📖 How this report works

What it shows

Sales posted in the date range by product tree category and product: quantity, sales amount, each product's share of the category and of the whole, and its average price, with charts per category and a Product Tree Summary at the end. It is the BI version of the standard Gross Sales By Product Tree report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Qty is in the product's own UOM (a line sold in another UOM is converted).
  • Sales Amount is qty × unit price (plus the line's adjustment per unit), in the home currency. Credit returns count negative when Include credit return items is ticked.
  • A product in two categories counts in both unless Show each product once is ticked (then only its first category).
  • Products in no category are left out, as in the standard report, unless Include products with no product tree is ticked.
  • Average Price = Sales Amount ÷ Qty.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Click a category's row to show or hide its products and charts; Expand / Collapse do every category.
  • Click a column heading to sort the products within each category; drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it.
  • Hover a bar or a pie slice for its figures.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
