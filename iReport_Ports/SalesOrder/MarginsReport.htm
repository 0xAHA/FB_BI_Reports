📖 How this report works

What it shows

What each product sold for, what it cost and the margin made, from the sales order lines posted (fulfilled) in the date range. It is the BI version of the standard Margins Report and reads the same data the same way.

Each line is one sales order's posted quantity of a product. Unit Price includes any positive adjustment on the line; quantities and prices are in the product's own unit of measure. COGS is the cost Fishbowl posted for the items.

Totals and margins
  • Margins = Total Price − COGS.
  • Margins % = Margins ÷ Total Price (0 when the price is 0 or less).
  • A group's margin % is its total margin over its total price, not the average of its lines' percentages.
  • Grouped by Product Tree, a product in two categories is listed and totalled under both, so the Grand Totals count it twice, as the standard report does.
  • Unit Cost and COGS need the Sales Order-Cost access right; Margins and Margins % need Sales Order-Margin.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Include returns adds Credit Return lines; Include shipping adds Shipping lines.
  • The Columns switches show or hide columns, as the standard report's column options do.
  • Picking a product tree category brings its sub-categories too.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each product (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Total Price, Qty, COGS, Margins or Margins % by any two of customer, product, product tree, sales order, salesperson, location group and UOM. Click a figure to see the lines behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch to the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so rows (whole groups) with a Load all button; the totals then cover the rows shown.
