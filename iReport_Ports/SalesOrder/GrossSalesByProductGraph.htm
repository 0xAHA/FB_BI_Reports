📖 How this report works

What it shows

The products that sold the most in the date range (by the value of the order lines fulfilled then), greatest first, with their quantity, a totals line and charts. It is the BI version of the standard Gross Sales By Product report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Sales is each line's fulfilled quantity times its unit price (plus its adjustment per unit), added up per product, in the home currency. Order-level discounts are separate lines without a product, so they aren't taken off.
  • Qty is the quantity fulfilled; with Include returns, credit returns count against it (and their negative price against Sales). A product whose net quantity isn't above zero isn't listed.
  • Orders without a salesperson, or whose salesperson is no longer a Fishbowl user, are not counted (as in the standard report).
  • The Totals line adds up the products listed.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away; the Layout boxes turn the table, the Qty column, the totals and each set of charts on or off.
  • Hover a bar or a pie slice for its figures. The pies show the first seven products and put the rest in "Other".
  • Click a column heading to sort (a third click for the report's order); drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it. Click a product to open it.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report: the table, then each set of charts on its own page. Print prints those pages.
