📖 How this report works

What it shows

How many of each product were shipped, grouped by location group, class, sales person or customer group. It is the BI version of the standard Sales By Count report and reads the same data the same way.

It counts sales order lines (Sale, Drop Ship and Kit) whose last fulfillment falls in the date range, with the whole quantity fulfilled on the line, in the product's own unit of measure.

Groups and filters
  • Customer Group: a customer in several of the chosen groups is counted under one of them only (the one it was added to last), as in the standard report. A customer in none is under No Group; pick No Group under Customer groups to keep only those.
  • Leave a filter at All (or empty) for no limit. Location groups are those of the order.
  • There are no totals: the quantities are in each product's own unit.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its products; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • Click a column heading to sort the products within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the Sort by order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the quantity by product and group (or UOM). Click a figure to see the lines behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole groups) with a Load all button.
