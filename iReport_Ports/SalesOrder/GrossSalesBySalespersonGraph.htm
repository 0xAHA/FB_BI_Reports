📖 How this report works

What it shows

Each salesperson's gross sales posted in the date range (the value of the sales order items fulfilled then), greatest first, with a bar chart and a pie chart. It is the BI version of the standard Gross Sales By Salesperson report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Sales adds up the posted value of sale, drop-ship, misc. credit, shipping and kit lines; misc. sales and credit returns (negative) only when their boxes are ticked. Amounts are in the home currency.
  • The salesperson is the one on the sales order. Orders without one, or whose salesperson is no longer a Fishbowl user, are not counted.
  • Only the first Number to show salespeople are listed.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Show data and Show graphs turn the table and the charts on or off.
  • Hover a bar or a pie slice for its figures. The pie shows the first seven salespeople and puts the rest in "Other".
  • Click a column heading to sort (a third click for the report's order); drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report: the table, then the charts. Print prints those pages.
