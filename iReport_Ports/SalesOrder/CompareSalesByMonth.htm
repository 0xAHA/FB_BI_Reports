📖 How this report works

What it shows

Each product's posted sales in two date ranges side by side: the quantity (in the product's own unit) and the price (home currency). By default last month against this month. It is the BI version of the standard Compare Sales By Month report and reads the same data the same way.

A sale counts on the order's posting date, for the orders of the chosen location groups.

What counts
  • Lines with a product: sales, drop ships, kits, misc. credits and shipping lines that carry a product. Credit returns are left out, as in the standard report.
  • Price = (unit price + the line's adjustment spread over its ordered quantity) × the quantity posted.
  • Show products with no sales lists every product, inactive ones included, with zeros.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Pick each period's quick range or type its dates.
  • Change columns adds how much the quantity and price moved from period 1 to period 2, and the price change in %.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The footer totals the prices (on screen only; the standard report prints no totals).
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: price or quantity by product and period. Click a figure to see the rows behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
