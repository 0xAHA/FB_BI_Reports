📖 How this report works

What it shows

The tax on the sales orders in a date range, order by order, totalled by location group, customer or tax rate. It is the BI version of the standard Sales Order Taxes report and works the tax out the same way.

How the tax is worked out
  • From the order's lines: each line's tax rate × its total price, for lines flagged taxable (on an Australian database, every line). Order Tax adds them up, unrounded; a line shows its tax rounded to the cent.
  • The total saved on the order is not used. It can be out of date (0, or an older figure, while the lines carry tax); tick Show saved order tax to see it beside the worked-out tax, marked where they differ by more than a cent.
  • Include line item tax adds Tax lines (a line of type Tax on the order) at their price.
  • Amounts are in the home currency.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Pick which date to filter on (scheduled, created, issued, completed, last modified or the line's last fulfilment).
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its orders; Expand / Collapse do every group. Show SO line items lists each order's lines.
  • Click a column heading to sort the orders within each group; drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot of the tax by any two of group, location group, customer, salesperson, tax rate, line tax code, status and date.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
