📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every discount line (a Discount Percentage or Discount Amount item) on a sales order that was fulfilled in the date range, grouped and totalled by the discount's name. It is the BI version of the standard Discount Summary report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Amount is the discount line's price with its sign turned, so a discount reads as a positive amount. A line with a positive price (a restocking fee entered as a discount) shows as a negative amount.
  • Only fulfilled discount lines count; Date is when the line was last fulfilled.
  • Amounts are in the home currency.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Discount name and SO number take % as a wildcard.
  • Click a discount's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every discount. Show detail off starts them all collapsed.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each discount (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the amount or the number of lines by any two of discount, customer, sales order and date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). Click a figure to see the lines behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole discounts) with a Load all button; the Grand Total still covers every line.
