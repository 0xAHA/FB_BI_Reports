📖 How this report works

What it shows

The sales order lines (sale, drop-ship and kit lines) on SOs issued in the date range whose price is not the product's own list price: the SO price, the product price and the difference, red when the line was sold below list. It is the BI version of the standard Price Discrepancy report and reads the same data the same way.

The prices

SO Price is the line's unit price with any discount or adjustment on the line spread over its quantity. Product Price is the price on the product record, before any pricing rule. A line is listed when its unit price (before the adjustment) differs from the product price, so a line can show a difference of 0.00 when only its discount differs.

Kit lines (a kit and the items in it) are listed only when their SO price is not zero.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each SO was issued; both end days count in full. Choosing no customers or salespeople means all of them.
  • Click an SO, customer or product to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the line difference (difference × the line's quantity), the average difference, the prices, the quantity or the number of lines by any two of customer, salesperson, product, status and date issued (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on the total line difference by customer. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 lines with a Load all button.
