📖 How this report works

What it shows

One sales order as the customer document: the BI version of the standard Sales Order Report and Sales Invoice, reading the same data the same way. The title follows the stock rules: Sales Order, Estimate, Invoice, or (Australia) Tax Invoice and Credit Adj Note.

  • The App view is the order on screen: customer, addresses, dates and terms, the lines with kit items under their kit, the totals, and payments and memos when those options are on.
  • The PDF view is the printed document, page by page, as the standard report prints it.
Choosing the order
  • Open Filters and type an SO number, a customer or a customer PO: the list shows the matching orders with their status, created date and total. The status and Created chips narrow it; with the box empty (or the ▾ button) it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next order within those chips.
  • The report opens on the order you looked at last (else the newest). When the BI report's Sales_Order setting names an order, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a sales order (the Sales Order module's Apps menu), the report shows that order and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (document, header, addresses, sections, lines, totals) and Settings (the info strip and the line columns); changes apply straight away. The standard report's saved options are the starting point; Reset options goes back to them. Invoice prints the Sales Invoice instead.
  • Click an SO, customer or product number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a line heading to sort the lines (again to reverse, a third time for line order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The printed document always keeps the standard columns in line order.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this view and the printable pages. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the lines with their prices and totals.
