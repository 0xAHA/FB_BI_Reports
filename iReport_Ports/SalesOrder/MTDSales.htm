📖 How this report works

What it shows

A month's posted sales, day by day: a bar chart and a line chart of each day's amount, and the month's Total to Date. It is the BI version of the standard MTD Sales report and reads the same data the same way.

A sale counts on the day its order was posted. Every posted line counts (any item type, any location group); credit returns count with their negative amount.

The figures
  • Amount = (unit price + the line's adjustment spread over its ordered quantity) × the quantity posted, in your home currency.
  • The charts always run from day 1 to day 31, as the standard report's do; days with no sales (and day 31 of a shorter month) are 0.
  • Total to Date is the whole month's total.
Using it
  • Pick the month under Filters; changes apply straight away. Title replaces "Month to Date Sales" on the page.
  • Hover a bar or a point to see its day and amount. The table under the charts lists each day with a running Month to date total.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable page, laid out like the standard report: the two charts on one page. Daily figures in the PDF adds the table on a second page. Print prints those pages; Excel saves the daily figures.
