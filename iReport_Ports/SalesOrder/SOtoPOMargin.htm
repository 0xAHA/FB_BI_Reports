📖 How this report works

What it shows

Sales orders that have purchase orders raised against them, with each SO's sale, the cost of its POs and the profit. It is the BI version of the standard SO To PO Margin report and reads the same data the same way.

Where the figures come from

A purchase order counts for a sales order when its Customer SO field is the SO's number. Voided POs are left out.

Sale: the SO's total of its sale, drop-ship, kit, associated-price, discount and shipping lines (not credits or tax). Cost: each PO's total cost. Profit: the sale less the cost of all the SO's POs.

Customer is the SO's customer contact, as the standard report prints it; More columns adds the customer's name.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the SO's date created. Choosing no salespeople means all of them; untick a status to leave its orders out.
  • Sort by orders the SOs (by their first PO for PO and PO date); each SO stays together.
  • Cost needs the Sales Order-Cost right and Profit the Sales Order-Margin right (or admin); without them those columns are left out everywhere.
  • Click an SO's row to hide or show its POs, or use Expand / Collapse. Click an SO column's heading to sort the SOs, a PO column's to sort the POs within each SO (again to reverse, a third time for the report's order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag its right edge to resize it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: sale, cost, profit or margin % by any two of salesman, customer, SO status and SO date, one row per SO. Click a figure to see the SOs behind it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
