📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every payment taken on a sales order in the date range, grouped by payment method (cash, check, credit card…), with a subtotal for each method and a grand total: the day's (or the period's) takings by tender. It is the BI version of the standard Z Report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • A payment is one entered in the sales order's Payment dialog; a refund is a negative payment and reduces the totals.
  • The date is when the payment was taken (hover a date for the time), not the order's date.
  • Register is the order's point-of-sale register; orders without one count as "No Register".
  • As in the standard report, "All classes" leaves out orders with no class, and choosing salespeople still keeps orders that have no salesperson.
  • Amounts are in the home currency.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Sort by orders the payments within each method.
  • Click a payment method's row to show or hide its payments; Expand / Collapse do every method. Show group headers, Show totals and Show timestamp are the standard report's options.
  • Click a column heading to sort the payments within each method (a third click for the Sort by order); drag a heading to move the column, its right edge to resize it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the amount by any two of payment method, date (by day, week, month…), customer, salesperson, register and sales order.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and printable pages laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
