📖 How this report works

What it shows

Posted sales of each product, month by month: for every product the price (top) and the quantity sold (below, in the product's unit), a Part Totals column and a Monthly Totals row. It is the BI version of the standard Sales By Month report and reads the same data the same way.

A sale counts in the month it was posted (the order's posting date), not the month it was ordered.

The figures
  • Price = (unit price + the line's adjustment spread over its ordered quantity) × the quantity posted, in your home currency.
  • Quantity is converted to the product's own unit of measure.
  • Only the checked item types count (Sale, Drop Ship and Kit by default, as the standard report). A line without a product (a misc. sale, a discount) is listed under the number typed on it.
  • Only months with sales get a column; a blank cell means no sales of that product that month.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The product number takes % as a wildcard (e.g. BK%).
  • Click a column heading to sort the products (a month or Part Totals sorts by its price; again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag Product or Description onto the other to swap them; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: price, quantity or posted lines by any two of product, month (or quarter, year), item type and UOM. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report: the months that don't fit across a page continue on the next pages. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so product-months with a Load all button; the totals then cover the products shown.
