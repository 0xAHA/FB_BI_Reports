📖 How this report works

What it shows

Open work orders (Entered or Started) whose finished good is short: the work order needs more than its location group has on hand. It is the BI version of the standard Production Demand report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Needed is the work order's quantity of the finished good, in the part's UOM. On Hand is all of that part's stock in the work order's location group. Short is the difference.
  • Customer Needed work orders were made for a sales order; Stock Needed ones were not.
  • Machine is the BOM's custom field named "Machine". Estimated Time is the BOM's estimated duration; Total Est. Time is that times the work order's quantity.
  • At the end, Finished Goods Short totals the list by part. With Show raw goods, Raw Goods Short lists the raw goods those work orders need more of than is on hand.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The date range is when the work order was created; "This month" is the standard report's default.
  • Show raw goods lists each work order's raw goods under it, with what is short, and adds the Raw Goods Short summary.
  • Click a location group's row to show or hide its work orders; Expand / Collapse do every group. Group rows count their work orders and total their Total Est. Time.
  • Click a column heading to sort the work orders within each need type (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); raw goods stay under their work order. The summaries' headings sort their parts. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Needed, On Hand, Short or the estimated hours by any two of location group, need type, customer, part, machine, work order, UOM and schedule date (by day, week, month…). Machine × week shows the hours booked on each machine. Click a figure to see the work orders behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so work orders (whole location groups) with a Load all button; the summaries then cover the work orders shown.
