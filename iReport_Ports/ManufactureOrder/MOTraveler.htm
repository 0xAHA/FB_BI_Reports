📖 How this report works

What it shows

The traveler of one manufacture order: its header (dates, location group, class, sales order), its note, and every work order with its BOM, quantity, status, dates, priority, location, assigned users and finished goods. It is the BI version of the standard Manufacture Order Traveler and reads the same data the same way.

With Add the WO travelers on, each work order's own traveler follows: its finished and raw goods, instructions and tracking write-ins.

Choosing the MO
  • Open Filters and type an MO number: the list shows up to 20 matches with their status, finished good and customer (with the box empty, the newest 20). Enter or a click opens one; the arrows step to the previous / next MO.
  • The report opens on the MO you looked at last (else the newest issued MO). When the BI report's Manufacture_Order setting names an MO, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a manufacture order (its Apps menu), the report prints that MO and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
  • An Entered MO has no work orders yet: the report says to issue it, as the standard one does.
Using it
  • Option changes apply straight away. Order by sets the work orders' order (Date Scheduled, WO # ascending or descending).
  • Tracking: while a work order is open its finished goods show a write-in line per tracking type (lot, use-by…); once fulfilled, the values recorded.
  • Click a WO #, part, sales order or the MO number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a Work Orders heading to sort the work orders (again to reverse, a third time for the chosen Order by). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The traveler tables stay in build order.
  • Settings holds the column switches of the standard report (header boxes, finished goods, the WO travelers' columns) and the two titles.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the work orders (one row per finished good) and, with the WO travelers on, every work order item.
