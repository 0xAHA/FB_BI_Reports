📖 How to Use This Report

What it shows

Available to Build is how many complete builds you could make right now from available stock, capped by the scarcest component (the constraint). "Available" means Available for Sale = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Drop Ship, as in Fishbowl's standard report.

Enter a Build qty (top right, default 1) and each BOM shows how many component lines are Short for that many builds and the Shortfall Cost of buying them. Type over a BOM row's own Build Qty to plan that BOM differently; changing the header figure resets them all.

Rows, lines and columns
  • Click ▸ beside a BOM (or its Constraint) to open its component lines. Yellow lines are short: Qty Needed is more than Available. A staged sub-assembly opens to its own components.
  • Unit Cost follows Settings › Cost basis. "avg" / "std" beside a cost says it fell back to average or standard cost; no cost means the part has none, and its shortfall is left out of the totals (marked †).
  • Settings › Layout › Side by side, plus Total gives each selected location group its own Available / Can Build / Short / Shortfall Cost columns, and a Total worked out on their combined stock.
  • Columns (top right) turns columns on and off and reorders them; drag a heading to move it, drag its edge to resize, click it to sort. Hover a heading for what it means.
  • The footer total adds each BOM's shortfall as if it were built on its own. BOMs that share parts compete for the same stock: Shortfall by part pools the BOMs you pick in Filters and nets each part's total need against stock once.
Filters, export & print

Filters picks Location Groups, BOMs (searchable; none picked = all), Product BOMs only (the finished good is a product), finished goods and constraint parts. Can build (the default) lists BOMs that cover the Build Qty; Short lists the ones that don't.

Excel / CSV export one row per component line with the BOM number and description on every row and a column per location group and figure, ready to filter and pivot. The App / PDF buttons switch to printable pages laid out like the standard report; Print prints them.
