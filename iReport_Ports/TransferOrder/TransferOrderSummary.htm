📖 How this report works

What it shows

Transfer orders in a date range: where each one goes from and to, its type and status, and how much was ordered, shipped and is still to go. It is the BI version of the standard Transfer Order Summary and reads the same data the same way.

The quantities

Ordered QTY is each line's quantity to fulfil. Shipped is its fulfilled quantity: a Ship transfer counts it once it has been received at the destination, so an order that is on its way still shows 0. Remaining is Ordered less Shipped. Group rows total their lines; the footer totals everything shown.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The date range is on the date you pick: Fulfillment Date (each line's scheduled date, the standard default) picks lines, so an order may show only some of them. The standard default, This month, shows only this month: pick All dates for everything. An order without the chosen date (e.g. not issued yet, with Date Issued) is never listed.
  • Group by TO number (one row per order) or part number (one row per part, its orders under it). Sort by orders the transfer orders (by part: each part's orders).
  • Show details lists every group's lines; off, the report is the summary rows alone, as the standard default. Click a group's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • The location group lists hold your own location groups; nothing picked means all of them. The Columns switches hide Ordered, Shipped or Remaining.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Grouped by TO number every heading sorts the orders, and Ordered / Shipped / Remaining sort each order's lines too; grouped by part, Part # and the quantities sort the parts and every heading sorts each part's orders. Status sorts in workflow order. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Ordered, Shipped, Remaining, the number of lines or of transfer orders by any two of From LG, To LG, TO #, part, status, type, Date Issued and Fulfillment Date. It opens on From LG × To LG: how many orders run between each pair of location groups. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole orders, or whole parts) with a Load all button; the footer total then covers the lines shown.
