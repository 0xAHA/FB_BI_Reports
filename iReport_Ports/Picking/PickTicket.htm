📖 How this report works

What it shows

The ticket for one pick: the parts to pick, where to get them and where to put them, with the pick's header and orders. It is the BI version of the standard Pick Ticket and Pick Report (and the Pick Report with SO custom fields) in one report: same data, same layout, every option of both.

Show items picks the list:

  • Still to pick: every item that isn't Finished: Entered, Short, Hold, Started, Committed. This is the one to print for a pick nobody has started yet.
  • Ready to pick (stock Pick Ticket): only Started items (and Committed with Committed items too) plus non-inventory lines, exactly as the standard Pick Ticket. A pick that isn't started prints no items in this mode.
  • All items (stock Pick Report) (default): everything on the pick, Finished included, with its status.

The Status column shows each item's status (not in Ready to pick). Short stands out in bold, Hold in italics.

Suggested locations
  • An item that isn't started yet (Entered, Short, Hold) has no location: Fishbowl only assigns the location and tag when the pick is started.
  • For those items the Location column suggests where the part is in stock in the pick's location group: up to three active, pickable locations with stock available (on hand less what picks have committed or, once started, already set aside there), the part's default location first. The figure is in the part's own UOM.
  • They are suggestions only, shown in italics as "suggest: …". The real location is the one Fishbowl assigns when the pick is started, which may differ. With no stock available the Location stays empty.
  • Turn them off with Suggest locations.
Choosing the pick
  • Open Filters and type a pick number, an order number or a customer: the list shows up to 20 matching picks with their orders, status and date. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next pick.
  • The report opens on the pick you looked at last (else the newest open pick in your location groups). When the BI report's Pick setting names a pick, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a pick (the Picking module's Apps menu), the report shows that pick and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard reports is in Filters (items, sort, header and orders) and Settings (title, custom fields); changes apply straight away. The standard reports' saved options are the starting point.
  • Kit components are grouped under their kit. Combine like items merges items with the same part, location, tag and destination.
  • Click a part, order or the pick number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click an item heading to sort the items within each kit (again to reverse, a third time for the report's sort). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • Settings → SO custom fields adds up to six sales-order custom fields under the orders and three SO-item fields under each part, with your own titles.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard ticket. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the items listed, with their suggested locations, tracking and notes.
