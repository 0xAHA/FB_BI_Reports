📖 How this report works

What it shows

Your products with their pictures, prices and barcodes, by product tree category: a catalog to print and hand out. It is the BI version of the standard Product Catalog V2, with every option of the first Product Catalog as well.

A product in two categories is in both; one in none is under Uncategorized, last.

Three views
  • Catalog: cards by category. Its printout is the full catalog: cover page, table of contents, the cards four across with each category from a new page, and the order sheet.
  • Table: one line per product (the first Product Catalog's layout).
  • Order sheet: number, description, UOM and a blank quantity, for customers to fill in. Printed two columns a page.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Each product shows switches the picture, price, SKU, UPC and the barcodes (product number, SKU, UPC: any of them). Printed catalog switches the cover, contents and order sheet.
  • Click a category heading to collapse it, or use Expand / Collapse. A collapsed category is left out of the printed catalog and order sheet; the Table view prints its heading.
  • Click a product number to open it in the Product module.
  • In the Table and Order sheet views, click a column heading to sort the products within each category (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); drag a heading onto another to move the column, or its right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages for the view you are in.
  • Pictures are the products' own (the thumbnails Fishbowl keeps). Settings → Pictures can use the part's picture instead when a product has none.
  • UPCs print as UPC-A barcodes (13 digits as EAN-13); product numbers and SKUs as Code 128.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so products (whole categories) with a Load all button.
