📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every active product with the price set on the product itself. It is the BI version of the standard Product Price List and reads the same data the same way.

Pricing rules are not applied: a customer may pay a different price. The Customer Price List shows one customer's price after their rules.

Categories
  • Group by Product Tree lists the products under each product tree category, by name; products in no category come first, under No Product Tree. A product in two categories is listed under each.
  • Group by None lists every product once, by product number.
  • Picking a category in Product tree brings its sub-categories too, as picking a node in Fishbowl's product tree does.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Product number, UPC and SKU match the whole value, in any case; % is a wildcard (ABC% = starts with ABC, %ABC% = contains ABC). Blank = all.
  • Click a category's row to show or hide its products; Expand / Collapse do them all.
  • Click a column heading to sort the products within each category (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the average, lowest or highest Price, a count of products or of lines, by any two of category, parent category and product. A product in two categories counts in each. Click a figure to see the products behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large list shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole categories) with a Load all button.
