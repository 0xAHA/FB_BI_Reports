📖 How this report works

What it shows

What received purchase-order lines cost once their bills were reconciled, PO by PO: what the PO said they would cost (Original), what the vendor billed (Billed) and what they cost once freight and other landed costs were added (Landed). It is the BI version of the standard Reconcile Report and reads the same data the same way.

The columns

Original Total is the received quantity times the PO line's unit cost; Billed and Landed Total come from the reconciled receipt line. They are in the home currency. The Currency columns give the same amounts in the vendor's currency (a vendor without a currency shows home amounts).

Billed is red when it is more than Original and green when less.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each line was reconciled; both end days count in full. PO and part numbers match exactly unless you use % as a wildcard (%100% finds every number containing 100).
  • Which lines: the standard report lists PO lines that have a part. Tick PO lines without a part for misc. purchases, and Cost items added in Reconcile for the freight and fees entered while reconciling (they have no PO line; their Original is 0). Both count in the PO and grand totals when ticked.
  • Columns switches columns on and off, as the standard report's options do. A multi-currency company starts with the Currency columns, others with the home ones.
  • Click a PO's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every PO. A PO's row carries its totals.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each PO (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Original, Billed, Landed or the Billed − Original / Landed − Original difference (home currency) by any two of vendor, PO, currency, part, line type and date reconciled (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on Billed − Original by vendor per month. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole POs) with a Load all button; the totals then cover the lines shown.
