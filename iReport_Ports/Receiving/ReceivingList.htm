📖 How this report works

What it shows

The receiving list for one receipt: the parts to receive on a purchase, sales or transfer order, the part's default location, the quantity to receive and, once received, the quantity received. It is the BI version of the standard Receiving List: same data, same layout, the same options.

  • Qty To Receive is the receipt line's quantity. Qty Received fills in once the line is Received or Fulfilled; the printed list leaves a line under it to write on.
  • Under each item: its tracking (lot, serial, …) and the order line's note.
  • Reject / return and scrapped lines are not listed, as in the standard report.
Choosing the receipt
  • A receipt is named by its order, with the letter the Receiving module uses: P5001 (purchase order), S10042 (sales order), T5 (transfer order).
  • Open Filters and type an order number, a vendor, a customer or a location group: the list shows up to 20 matching receipts with their status and how many lines are still to receive. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next receipt.
  • The report opens on the receipt you looked at last (else the newest one still to receive in your location groups). When the BI report's Receipt setting names a receipt, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a receipt (the Receiving module's Apps menu), the report shows that receipt and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Filters holds every option of the standard report: include received items, sort by, the Vendor Part # and Tracking Num columns, the order barcode and a part number barcode per item. Changes apply straight away; the standard report's saved options are the starting point.
  • Click the receipt number, the order, the vendor / customer or a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click an item heading to sort the items (again to reverse, a third time for Sort by). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the items listed, with their tracking and notes.
