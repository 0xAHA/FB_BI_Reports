📖 How this report works

What it shows

Everything received in the date range: every received line of a purchase order (vendor receipts) and of a sales order credit return (customer receipts), with its order, quantity and cost. It is the BI version of the standard Receiving History and reads the same data the same way.

Cost and totals

Item Cost is the order line's cost for the quantity received; Landed Cost the receipt line's landed cost (freight and other costs added at reconcile). Both are in the home currency. Customer receipts are returns, so their cost is negative.

Each group and vendor / part row totals its cost and, with Show quantity totals, its quantity. Quantities are added up as received, whatever their units, as in the standard report; a row shows the UOM only when all its lines share one. The title band (and the PDF) gives the Total Cost of everything listed.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each line was received; both end days count in full.
  • Picking vendors leaves out customer receipts, and picking customers leaves out vendor receipts, as in the standard report. Pick both to see both, each filtered. Include misc items adds purchase order lines with no part (misc purchases).
  • Lines with no location (non-inventory items) show whatever location groups are picked, as in the standard report.
  • Click a group's or a vendor / part's row to show or hide what is under it; Expand shows every line, Collapse leaves the totals only.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each vendor / part (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: qty, item cost or landed cost (or the number of lines) by any two of receipt type, location group, class, vendor / customer, part, UOM, order and date received (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on cost by vendor / customer per month. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole groups) with a Load all button; the totals then cover the lines shown.
