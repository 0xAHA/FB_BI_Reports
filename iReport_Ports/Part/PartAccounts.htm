📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every part with the accounts it posts to: COGS, Asset (inventory), Income (from the part's product), Adjustment and Scrap. Use Default means no account is assigned, so the company's default account is used. It is the BI version of the standard Part Accounts report and reads the same data the same way.

One row per part

A part is listed once, unless its products carry different income accounts: then it is listed once per income account. A part without a product shows Income Use Default.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. With no filters it lists every part, as the standard report.
  • Accounts → Show narrows the list to the parts that have an account of their own, or to those on the defaults only.
  • Group by an account to see which parts use each one. Click an account's row to show or hide its parts; Expand / Collapse do every account.
  • Click a part number to open it in the Part module.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); grouped, the parts sort within each account. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the number of parts by any two of the five accounts, part type, active / inactive and account assignment. COGS account × part type shows which kinds of part post where. Click a figure to see the parts behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 rows (whole accounts when grouped) with a Load all button.
