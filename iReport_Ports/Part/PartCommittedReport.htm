📖 How this report works

What it shows

Where each part's stock is committed: how much is already reserved for every open transfer, purchase, work and sales order, in the part's own unit, with the lots and serial numbers picked. It is the BI version of the standard Part Committed Report and reads the same data the same way.

Committed, allocated, not available

Committed stock is reserved for an order: on a committed pick, packed on a shipment, or on its way. It is part of the order's Allocated demand (the Part Allocated report), which also counts what nobody has picked yet. Neither is Not Available stock, which sits in a location that doesn't count as available.

  • Transfer orders: committed picks, packed shipments and stock shipped but not yet received.
  • Purchase orders: credit-return and out-sourced lines picked but not yet shipped back.
  • Work orders: raw goods picked for entered and started work orders, until they are used.
  • Sales orders: committed picks and packed shipments not yet shipped.
Using it
  • Filters → Part number: pick one part, type a pattern with % (e.g. B2%), or leave it empty for every part with committed stock. Opened from a part (its Apps menu) the report shows that part and it can't be changed. Show tracking lists each order's lots, dates and serial numbers.
  • A part's row carries its Total Committed; each order-type row totals its orders. Click a part's row to show or hide its orders; Expand / Collapse do every part. Click an order or part number to open it.
  • Click a column heading to sort the orders within their order type (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty or count the orders by any two of part, order type, order and UOM (part × order type to start). Click a figure to see the orders behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report: each part with its four order lists side by side. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so orders (whole parts) with a Load all button.
