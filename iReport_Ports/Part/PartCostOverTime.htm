📖 How this report works

What it shows

How a part's cost has moved: every part that got stock (a cost layer: a receipt, a finished build, an adjustment in) in the date range, with its costs and a chart of each layer's unit cost. It is the BI version of the standard Part Cost Over Time report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Std. Cost: the part's standard cost.
  • First / Last Cost: the unit cost of the part's first and latest cost layer ever.
  • Low / High Cost: the lowest and highest unit cost over all its layers, as the standard report. The chart's caption gives the range's own low and high.
  • Avg. Cost: the average cost Fishbowl captured overnight on or before the range's end date (the latest capture of the part). n/a = no capture yet, e.g. stock first received today.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Part number takes % as a wildcard.
  • Click a part's line to show or hide its chart; Expand / Collapse do every part (remembered). Hover a chart for each layer's date, unit cost and quantity. Click a part number to open it in the Part module.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts (again to reverse, a third time for part order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot of the cost layers: by default each part's weighted unit cost (total cost ÷ quantity) per month. Pick another date grouping, measure (layer unit cost average / lowest / highest, quantity, total cost, layer count) or dimension; click a figure to see the layers behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report: a page per part with its chart. A collapsed part prints without its chart; with every chart collapsed the pages are a one-line-per-part list. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Opened from the Part, Inventory or Product module's Apps menu, the report starts on that part.
  • A very large result shows the parts whose layers fit in the first 10,000, with a Load all button.
