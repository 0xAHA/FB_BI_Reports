📖 How this report works

What it shows

Each inventory part's reorder point (when stock falls to it, reorder) and order up to level (how high to reorder to). It is the BI version of the standard Reorder Points report and reads the same data the same way.

Quantities are in each part's own unit of measure, so there are no column totals.

Company wide or by location group
  • A part's level is either Company Wide (one level for the whole company) or for one location group.
  • Fishbowl uses one kind at a time, as set in the Location Group module's options. The note under Levels in the filters says which. A level of the other kind is kept but not used: it is marked not in use, and Show levels not in use off hides it.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Show parts without reorder points adds every other part, with blank levels.
  • Click a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The view selector next to Filters: By location group lists the parts under Company Wide and each group's name (click a heading to show or hide its parts; columns then sort within each group). Pivot shows the reorder points, order-up-to levels or a row count by any two of part, location group, in use, part status and UOM: part × location group compares a part's levels side by side. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • Opened for a part from a Fishbowl module, the report starts filtered to that part. Clear the Part number filter to see every part.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large catalogue shows its first 10,000 or so rows (whole parts) with a Load all button.
