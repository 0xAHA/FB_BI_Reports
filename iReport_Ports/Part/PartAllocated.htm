📖 How this report works

What it shows

Where each part is allocated: how much of it every open transfer, purchase, work and sales order still needs, in the part's own unit. It is the BI version of the standard Part Allocated report and reads the same data the same way.

Allocated, committed, not available

Allocated is open order demand: what orders still need, picked or not. Committed stock (the Part Committed Report) is the part of it already reserved on a pick or a packed shipment. Neither is Not Available stock, which sits in a location that doesn't count as available.

  • Transfer orders: still to send.
  • Purchase orders: credit-return and out-sourced lines, which send the part out.
  • Work orders: raw goods still to use (target − used) on entered and started work orders.
  • Sales orders: still to ship on issued and in-progress orders. Include drop-ship items adds drop-ship lines.
Using it
  • Filters → Part number: pick one part, type a pattern with % (e.g. B2%), or leave it empty for every part with open orders. Opened from a part (its Apps menu) the report shows that part and it can't be changed.
  • A part's row totals every order type; each order-type row totals its orders. Click a part's row to show or hide its orders; Expand / Collapse do every part. Click an order or part number to open it.
  • Click a column heading to sort the orders within their order type (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty or count the orders by any two of part, order type, order and UOM (part × order type to start). Click a figure to see the orders behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report: each part with its four order lists side by side. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so orders (whole parts) with a Load all button.
