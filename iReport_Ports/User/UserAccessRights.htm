📖 How this report works

What it shows

Who can use Fishbowl and what they can do in it. A user's rights come from the user groups they belong to (Setup › User Groups): a right is granted when any of their groups grants it. This report is the standard User Access Rights and User Login Log reports in one.

The views
  • Users: every user, their groups and location groups, how many rights they have, their last login and whether MFA is set. Click a user for their rights by module and which group grants each.
  • Groups: every user group, its members and rights. Click a group for its rights.
  • Compare: pick users and groups under Filters › Compare; they sit side by side, group membership first, then every right. ✓ is granted, — is not. Differences only hides the rows where they all agree.
  • Login log: logins and logouts between two dates (this month to start), for any users, by date or by user.
Good to know
  • A group has a right when it is ticked for the group in Setup › User Groups. The admin user has every right.
  • Show rights not granted also lists the rights other groups have that this user or group doesn't (greyed).
  • Report rights are the standard reports a group may run; BI Report rights are its BI report access.
  • Click a module's row to show or hide its rights; Expand / Collapse do them all. The App / PDF buttons switch to the printable pages; Print and Excel follow the view on screen.
