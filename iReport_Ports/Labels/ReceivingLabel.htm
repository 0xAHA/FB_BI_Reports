📖 How this report works

What it prints

One receipt's received items on a 4 x 6 in label: the BI version of the standard Receiving Label (Labels). Each item gets its part number, quantity, description, location, a barcode of the part and of its location, its note, up to three tracking values as barcodes and a barcode for every serial number. The order's barcode heads every label, and a long receipt runs onto as many labels as it needs.

The preview shows one label at a time, as Fishbowl's report viewer. Page through with the arrows above it.

The label
  • The pills under the header are the label sizes this report prints on: one label per page, at least 3 in wide and 4 in tall (4 × 6 in to start). They are shared with every label report.
  • Sheet setup changes the label's size and margins and saves it as your own (an admin can add it for everyone). From a Word template makes one from a label maker's Word template.
  • Print prints the labels at their size with no margins: in the print dialog choose the label printer and scale 100% (actual size).
Receipt and options
  • Receipt: type an order number (P / S / T in front is fine), a vendor, a customer or a location group; the chips filter the list and the arrows step through the receipts. Opened from the Receiving module the label shows that receipt and the picker is hidden.
  • Item: every item (as the standard label), or just one.
  • Options: every option of the standard label, starting from its saved options. The standard label prints only received and fulfilled items; Also items not yet received adds the entered and reconciled ones.
