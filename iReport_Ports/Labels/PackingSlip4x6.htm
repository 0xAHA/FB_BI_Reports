📖 How this report works

What it prints

One shipment's packing slip on a 4 x 6 in label: the BI version of the standard Packing Slip 4x6 label (Labels). Sales order, purchase order and transfer order shipments each get their own layout, and a long shipment runs onto as many labels as it needs (Page 1 of 3).

The preview shows one label at a time, as Fishbowl's report viewer. Page through with the arrows above it.

The label
  • The pills under the header are the label sizes this report prints on: one label per page, at least 3 in wide and 4 in tall (4 × 6 in to start). They are shared with every label report: one set up for Part Barcodes shows here too when it suits.
  • Sheet setup changes the label's size and margins and saves it as your own (an admin can add it for everyone). From a Word template makes one from a label maker's Word template.
  • Print prints the labels at their size with no margins: in the print dialog choose the label printer and scale 100% (actual size).
Shipment and options
  • Shipment: type a shipment number, an SO / PO / TO number or a ship-to name; the chips filter the list and the arrows step through it. Opened from a shipment (the Shipping module's Apps menu) the label shows that shipment and the picker is hidden.
  • Options: every option of the standard report, starting from its saved options. Layout: Standard (one item list and the carton list) or By Carton (the items under each carton, the header on every label).
