📖 How this report works

What it prints

A mailing label for each address: the customer's or vendor's name, the street, the city, state and zip, and the country if you want it. It replaces the standard Customer Address Labels and Vendor Address Labels.

Print (left): customers or vendors. Every customer prints every address of the Address type picked (All, Ship To, Bill To, Remit To, Home, Main Office), for the Customer groups picked (none picked: every group). Customers in no group are included too, unless Only customers in a group is ticked. A customer named Point of Sale is never printed, as in the standard report.

Two addresses with the same name and the same text print once. Inactive customers and vendors print too, as in the standard reports.

Ones I choose prints the addresses of the customers or vendors you pick, each with its own copies. Copies of each address repeats every label.

The preview shows one page at a time, as Fishbowl's report viewer: a sheet, or one label on a label printer. Page through with the arrows above it.

The sheet
  • The pills under the header are the sheets and label printers. Pick the one in the printer.
  • Sheet setup changes the paper, how many labels across and down, the page margins and the gaps between labels, and saves it as your own setup (an admin can add it for everyone). Measure your sheet with a ruler, or use the numbers on its packet.
  • From a Word template: pick the label maker's Word template for your sheet (an Avery .doc or .docx), or drop the file anywhere on this page. Its page size, margins, label size and gaps fill a new sheet setup; check it with a Test sheet, then save it.
  • Test sheet prints numbered outlines of every label: hold it against a sheet of labels to the light to check it lines up.
  • Start at label skips used labels on a part-used sheet. Fill: across fills each row first; down fills each column first, as the standard sheets did.
The label
  • Each line is a field: the name, the street (a street on several lines uses Street line 2 and 3), the city, state and zip, and the country. Show country is the Country field's tick. Attention (the address's recipient) is there to add; the standard labels don't print it.
  • A line with nothing in it (no second street line, no country) takes no room.
  • Each field takes its Height share of the label. The lines print at 10 pt, as the standard labels; long text shrinks and wraps onto the Lines allowed. Auto makes a line as large as its room allows. Tick Fixed to keep the size picked: what doesn't fit is cut short with …
  • Bold and alignment are per field; untick a field to leave it off. Drag a field by its handle (☰, on the left) to change the order.
  • The preview zooms with − / + or Ctrl + mouse wheel. 100% is life size on a standard display (single labels open at it); Fit shows the whole page.
  • Print pages prints part of the job. A big job (hundreds of pages; on a label printer every label is a page) is flagged under Printing with how long it may take; Print in parts sends it in pieces, setting up the next part after each.
  • Print opens the labels in a print window, one sheet per page at the sheet's size. In the print dialog choose the label printer or the paper tray with the labels, scale 100% (actual size), no margins.
