📖 How this report works

What it prints

Part barcode labels that carry the part's tracking too: the part number and its barcode, and the lot, serial number, date or other primary tracking value with its own barcode. It replaces the standard Part Barcodes With Tracking Avery and One Off labels and the PO and WO Part Barcodes With Tracking 4x6 labels.

Print (left):

  • Parts at locations: for each part at each location, a label for each tracking value there (each serial number, each lot or date on each tag); a part without tracking gets a label for each unit at the location. Quantity per tracking number prints that many instead. Skip / How many cut each part and location's labels.
  • A purchase order: a 4 × 6 label for each part and tracking value received on the PO, with the quantity received (1 for a serial number).
  • A work order: the same for the finished goods of a work order, with the quantity made.

The location label and the 4 × 6 order label each keep their own sheet and fields: switching Print switches both.

The sheet
  • The pills under the header are the sheets and label printers. Pick the one in the printer.
  • Sheet setup changes the paper, how many labels across and down, the page margins and the gaps between labels, and saves it as your own setup (an admin can add it for everyone).
  • From a Word template: pick the label maker's Word template for your sheet (an Avery .doc or .docx), or drop the file anywhere on this page, then check it with a Test sheet.
  • Test sheet prints numbered outlines of every label: hold it against a sheet of labels to the light to check it lines up.
  • Start at label skips used labels on a part-used sheet. Fill: across fills each row first; down fills each column first, as the standard sheets did.
The label
  • Tracking name: as the standard label (the short name, Lot#, on the location label; the full name, Lot Number, on the 4 × 6), or always one of them. Dates print in the company's report date format, in the text and the barcode.
  • Each field takes its Height share of the label. Text is as large as its lines allow (Auto), or at most the size picked; long text shrinks and wraps onto the Lines allowed. Tick Fixed to keep the size picked. A field with nothing to print (no tracking) leaves its room to the others.
  • The barcode's type: Code 128 (the standard reports' barcode; any text), Code 39, EAN-13 / UPC-A, QR Code and Data Matrix. A value a type can't carry shows a red box instead, and is listed under Printing.
  • Bold and alignment are per field; untick a field to leave it off. Drag a field by its handle (☰, on the left) to change the order.
  • The preview zooms with − / + or Ctrl + mouse wheel. 100% is life size on a standard display; Fit shows the whole page.
  • Print pages prints part of the job; a big job is flagged under Printing, and Print in parts sends it in pieces.
  • Print opens the labels in a print window, one sheet per page at the sheet's size. In the print dialog choose the label printer or the paper tray with the labels, scale 100% (actual size), no margins.
