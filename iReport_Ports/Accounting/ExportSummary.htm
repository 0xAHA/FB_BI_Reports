📖 How this report works

What it shows

The sales, purchase and work order transactions Fishbowl has exported to your accounting system, or is waiting to export. It is the BI version of the standard Accounting Export Summary and reads the same data the same way.

Work order amounts are the adjustments made, not the final cost of the work order.

Status

Posted exported to accounting.

Not Posted waiting for the next export.

Marked as Posted marked as posted in Fishbowl without being sent.

Dates
  • Date Posted: when it was exported. A transaction that isn't posted yet has no Date Posted, so it only appears when the filter uses Item Date Fulfilled.
  • Item Date Fulfilled: when the items were shipped, received or built.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The view selector picks the section; Print and Excel cover every section shown.
  • Each order's row carries its totals; click it to show its lines. Collapse is the standard report with Show Details off.
  • Click a column heading to sort (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). The orders sort by the figure their row carries (Order #, Date Posted, the totals); the lines sort within their order. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Each section remembers its own layout; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • Pivot (in the view selector) totals any figure by any two of section, order, customer / vendor / BOM, product / part, line type, status and date posted. It opens on each section's total by status: what is posted and what is still waiting. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large section shows its first 10,000 lines (whole orders only, so every order's totals are complete) with a Load all button; its totals then cover the orders shown.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report: every section shown, one after another. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • SO: Price is unit price × quantity posted; the order total adds tax at the order's rate on taxable lines. COGS is the cost posted for each line.
  • WO: the total is the finished goods' cost; raw goods are listed but not added.
