📖 How this report works

What it shows

Inventory on hand that carries an expiration date, by location group, with when each lot expires. It is the BI version of the standard On Hand by Expiration Date report and reads the same data the same way.

Each line is one part in one location with one set of tracking values (one lot), its quantity summed across its tags, in the part's own unit.

Status
  • Valid expires in more than 30 days.
  • Expiring expires within the next 30 days.
  • Expired the expiration date is today or past.

Days are counted from today's date on the Fishbowl database server.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The date range is on the expiration date; pick All dates for every lot that has one.
  • Click a location group's row to show or hide its lots; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • Click a part number to open it in Inventory.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lots within each location group (again to reverse, a third time for the Order by order). Status sorts by days to expiry. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty, or count lots, by any two of location group, location, part, status, UOM and expiration date (by month, quarter…). Part by expiry month shows what runs out when. Click a figure to see the lots behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 lots with a Load all button.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Parts without an expiration-date tracking type are not listed.
