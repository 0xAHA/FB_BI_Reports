📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every quantity scrapped in Fishbowl: what, where from, who did it, and the customer/job it was charged to. It is the BI version of the standard Consumption Report and reads the same data the same way.

Total Cost is the part's cost at the time of the scrap × the quantity consumed.

Dates
  • Date Recorded: when the scrap was entered.
  • Back/Post Date: the date it was entered for. They differ when a scrap is back-dated.

The Date column shows whichever one the filter uses.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • With Show totals on, each group's row carries its totals; click it to show or hide its scraps.
  • Summary adds a per-part roll-up; pick it in the view selector. Print and Excel follow the Summary option.
  • Click a column heading to sort the scraps within each group, or the Summary's parts (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • Pivot in the view selector totals any figure (Total Cost, Qty Consumed…) by any two of part, customer/job, user, location group, location, UOM and the date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). Click a figure to see the scraps behind it. In Pivot view, Print, PDF and Excel give the pivot.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 scraps with a Load all button; totals then cover the scraps shown.
  • Quantity totals add up different units when parts are stocked in different units, exactly as the standard report does.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
Accounting side

The Adjustment Report lists the same scraps as accounting postings. Its Scrap rows total the same as this report (give or take rounding) when both cover the same scraps: pick Back/Post Date here to match its date range, and include inactive parts and every location group.
