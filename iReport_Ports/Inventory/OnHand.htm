📖 How this report works

What it shows

The quantity on hand of every inventory part, by location group, location and tracking (lot, date…). It is the BI version of the standard On Hand report and reads the same data the same way.

Each line is one part in one location with one set of tracking values. Qty is in the part's own unit of measure.

Options
  • Show parts with zero quantity lists every inventory part in every chosen location group, with 0 where it has no stock.
  • Show parts without tracking off keeps only parts that have tracking.
  • Tracking contains finds lines whose tracking includes the text, e.g. a lot number or a date written 2026-01-31.
  • Show serial numbers lists a serialized part's serial numbers under its line, in the Tracking column (so that column must be on). Click a line's serial numbers link to fold them.
  • Group by lays the table out three ways: Location group (the standard report); Part: one row per part across the chosen location groups with its total quantity (one part has one unit, so it adds up), its lines showing the location group, location and tracking; Location: one row per location with its line and part counts, the parts there underneath, for counting a location. Sort by applies to the Location group layout. Print, PDF and Excel follow the layout.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The column switches, titles and report title don't re-run the query.
  • Click a group's row (location group, part or location) to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • Click a part number to open it in the Inventory module.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). A line's serial numbers move with it. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Qty or a count of lines by any two of location group, location, part, ABC, UOM and the custom-field columns shown. Part by location group shows where each part is. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result (zero quantities on a big database) shows its first 10,000 lines with a Load all button.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • There are no quantity totals across parts, as in the standard report: different parts and units don't add up. The Part layout totals each part on its own.
