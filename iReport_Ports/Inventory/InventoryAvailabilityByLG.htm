📖 How this report works

What it shows

How much of each active inventory part is on hand, promised and on its way, location group by location group. It is the BI version of the standard Inventory Availability by Location Group report and reads the same data the same way.

Every quantity is in the part's own stocking unit (the UOM column).

The columns
  • Allocated: still to go out on open orders (sales, vendor credit returns, work orders using the part, transfers).
  • Not Available: stock in locations not counted as available.
  • On Order: still to come in (purchase orders, customer returns, work orders making the part, transfers).
  • Committed: stock already committed to picks.
  • Available = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Drop Ship, never below 0.
  • Short = Allocated − (On Hand − Not Available), never below 0.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. With no location group picked you see all of yours.
  • Show parts with zero quantity off hides a part in a location group when its quantities add up to nothing.
  • Short lines only keeps just the rows with a Short above 0; a location group with no short part drops out. The header counts the rows shown and hidden. It doesn't reload anything, and Print, PDF, Excel and the Pivot follow it.
  • Click a location group's row to show or hide its parts; Expand / Collapse do them all.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts within each location group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total any quantity by any two of part, location group, UOM and shortage. It opens as each part across the location groups (with the part's total), so a part's stock everywhere is on one line. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 rows with a Load all button; the counts then cover the rows shown.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • There are no quantity totals, as in the standard report: parts are stocked in different units.
