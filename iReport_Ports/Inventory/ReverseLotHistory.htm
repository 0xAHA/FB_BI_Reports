📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every inventory event recorded against a lot, newest first, and for each one that belongs to a work order, the raw goods that work order consumed with their lots: what went into the lot. It is the BI version of the standard Reverse Lot History report and reads the same data the same way.

For the other direction (where a lot went) use Lot History.

The genealogy goes back level by level: when a raw-good lot was itself made by a work order, that work order's raw goods show under it, stepped in further, and so on back to the lots that were bought in. Filters → Levels to trace sets how far (1 is the standard report; the default 5). A work order already listed higher up is named with "raw goods listed above" rather than repeated; one that comes round again (a rework loop) is named but not followed.

Searching
  • Type a lot number, or part of one, and press Enter. Every event whose tracking values contain it is listed, so a short search can match several lots.
  • A part number narrows it to that part; % or * is a wildcard.
  • A part number on its own lists every lot event of that part.
  • Over 20,000 matching events, nothing is shown and a note asks for a narrower search; Load all in the note runs it anyway (it can take a while).
  • When the genealogy stops short, a note says so and how many work orders lie deeper: past the levels you set (Load all traces all ten), or before a level that would take it over 1,000 work orders (Load all loads it anyway).
Reading it
  • Qty is the event's change in the part's stocking UOM; a part's row carries the net of its events. On a raw good it is the quantity consumed, without its sign.
  • Customer is the SO's customer on a shipment; phone and email are the customer's.
  • Yield events are left out: they repeat a movement another event already records.
  • Click a part's row to show or hide its events; Expand / Collapse do every part. Click a part, an event's order or a customer to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the events within each part, say by Type or Customer (again to reverse, a third time for newest first). Each work-order event keeps its raw goods under it; they sort the same way. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty (or count events) by any two of level (the lot's own events or the raw goods), trace level, part, lot, type, order, customer and event date. Rows part + lot by level lists every raw-good lot and how much of it went in, once per work order, at every level traced. Click a figure to see the events behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
