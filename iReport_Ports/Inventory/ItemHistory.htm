📖 How this report works

What it shows

For each part, in each location group it has stock history in: what was on hand at the start and end of the range, and the purchases, sales and inventory adjustments in between. It is the BI version of the standard Item History and reads the same data the same way.

The part row
  • Start / End Qty: on hand in that location group just before the range starts / when it ends, in the part's unit.
  • Avg Cost: the part's current average cost.
  • Avg Price / Avg Margin: from everything sold in the range, in every location group: price per unit, and price less posted cost per unit.
The histories
  • Purchase: received PO lines, by the day they posted. Credit returns are negative.
  • Sales: fulfilled SO lines, by the day they posted. Sales are negative, credit returns positive.
  • Inventory adjustment: added, adjusted and scrapped stock, work order output and consumption, and stock moved to or from another location group, a row per day. Moves inside the group are left out: they don't change what it holds.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Part number is matched as typed; use % as a wildcard.
  • Click a part row to show or hide its history; Expand / Collapse do every part.
  • Order, vendor, customer and work order numbers open the record in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each history (adjustment days within their category); again to reverse, a third time for date order. The parts and sections keep their order. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • Pivot in the view selector switches to a pivot: total any figure (Qty, Cost, Price) by any two of part, location group, history, category, date (by day, week, month, quarter or year), document and vendor / customer. Qty by part and history shows each part's purchases, sales and adjustments side by side. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result (a long date range over many parts) shows its first parts, each with its whole history, with a Load all button.
Reconciliation view

Pick Reconciliation in the view selector: a row per part and location group, in the part's unit, that checks the histories against the stock. Start Qty + Purchases + Sales (negative, as in their history) + Adjustments (each category, then their sum) = Expected End. Gap = End Qty − Expected End: stock movement the three histories don't account for. A gap is highlighted; point at it for the sum.

  • Rows are grouped by location group. There are no quantity totals across parts: their units differ.
  • Only parts with a gap (Filters) narrows every view to those rows; switch to Table to read their histories.
  • The reconciliation needs all three histories, so it loads them whatever the Show options say. Those still decide what the Table view lists.
What a gap usually means
  • The order belongs to one location group, the stock moved in another (the usual reason). The Purchase and Sales histories file each line under the order's location group, as the standard report does, but a PO can be received into, and an SO shipped from, another group. E.g. a BP purchase order received into Off Site Storage shows −1,720 at BP and +1,720 at Off Site Storage: the two gaps cancel out.
  • Back-dated entries. Start and End Qty are the stock on the newest entry dated before the date. An entry made later but dated earlier carries the stock as it stood when it was made. E.g. a work order consumption entered on 1 April but dated 31 March, after that day's output was booked: Start Qty already holds the 1,670 made on 1 April, so the gap is −1,670.
  • Posted on another day than received. Purchases are dated by when the PO line posted, the stock by when it arrived; occasionally that is another month (received 2 March, posted 27 February). Sales post as they ship.
  • Unit conversions. A PO or SO line in another unit is converted with the part's UOM conversion (one for one when there is none); a missing or different conversion leaves a gap.
  • Movements none of the histories list, rarely: enabling or disabling tracking with a quantity.
  • Not a cause: moves inside a location group. Picks and bin moves (and their "Yield" log rows) record stock moving between two locations of the same group; they don't change what the group holds and are left out. Moves to another group are the Movement category.
