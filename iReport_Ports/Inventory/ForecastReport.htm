📖 How this report works

What it shows

For each inventory part in each location group: the stock on hand now, then every open order that will move it, in scheduled date order, with a Running Total of where stock will stand after each one. It is the BI version of the standard Forecast Report and reads the same data the same way.

A running total at or below zero is shown in red: that order would leave you short.

What counts
  • SO: the quantity still to ship goes out; a credit return comes in.
  • PO: the quantity still to receive comes in; a vendor return goes out.
  • WO: finished goods come in and raw goods go out, at the work order's full planned quantity.
  • TO: stock leaves the sending group and arrives at the receiving one.

Quantities are in the part's stocking unit. Qty On Hand is Fishbowl's On Hand for the part in that location group; when some of it is in locations not counted as available (a hold or receiving bin, say), the part's row says how much.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Part number takes % as a wildcard.
  • Only parts that run short keeps just the parts whose running total reaches zero or below at some point (the red figures), with all their orders; a location group without one drops out. The header says how many parts it hides. It doesn't reload anything, and Print, PDF, Excel and the Pivot follow it.
  • Click a part's row to show or hide its orders; click a location group to hide all its parts. Collapse leaves one row per part: on hand, net change and where it ends up.
  • Click an order or part number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • A part's orders always stay in date order: the running total depends on it. Clicking Qty Change, Running Total or Scheduled Fulfillment Date sorts the parts within each location group by their net change, where they end up, or the date they first run short (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total any figure by any two of location group, part, order type, order number and scheduled date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). Qty Change by part and month shows what each month brings in and takes out; Min of Running Total shows the lowest stock will fall. Click a figure to see the orders behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 order lines, whole parts only, with a Load all button.
