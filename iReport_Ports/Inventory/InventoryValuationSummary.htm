📖 How this report works

What it shows

The quantity of every part on hand, by location, and what it is worth at today's cost. It is the BI version of the standard Inventory Valuation Summary and reads the same data the same way.

Asset Value = Qty × Unit Cost. Unit Cost is the part's average cost, or its standard cost when Costing method is Standard Cost.

What counts
  • Every tag holding the part, including inactive parts and inactive location groups, as the standard report does.
  • Only your own location groups are listed.
  • A part with no Inventory Asset account set shows Default: your accounting system's default inventory account.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total any figure (Asset Value, Qty…) by any two of location group, location, part, asset account and UOM. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 lines with a Load all button; totals then cover the lines shown.
  • Grouped by part, the part's row totals its quantity. Grouped by location group only the value is totalled, because the parts are in different units.
Accounting side

The Asset Valuation by Account report values the same stock per Inventory Asset account. With today's date, inactive parts shown and every location group here, its Average Cost total matches this report's grand total.
