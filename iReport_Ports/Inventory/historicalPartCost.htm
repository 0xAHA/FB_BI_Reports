📖 How this report works

What it shows

What each inventory part was worth on a past date: its quantity and unit cost as Fishbowl recorded them that day, and the total. It is the BI version of the standard Historical Part Cost and reads the same data the same way.

Where the figures come from
  • Once a day a Fishbowl scheduled task records, for every part with stock, its quantity and its average, standard and next cost. The time is in the status line.
  • A part with no stock that day isn't recorded: tick Show parts with zero quantity to list it anyway, as N/A.
  • If the server was off that day nothing was recorded; the page offers the last day that was.
  • FIFO Cost and LIFO Cost both use the recorded next cost (the cost of the next layer to be used), as the standard report does.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The costing method and the vendor column only redraw.
  • Click a column heading to sort by it (again to reverse, a third time for part number order); N/A figures sort last. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total any figure (Total Cost, Quantity…) by default vendor and / or UOM, for example what the stock from each vendor was worth that day. Click a figure to see the parts behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • The total adds every part's quantity × unit cost.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 parts with a Load all button; the total then covers the parts shown.
