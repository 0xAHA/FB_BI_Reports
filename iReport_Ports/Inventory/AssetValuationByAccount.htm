📖 How this report works

What it shows

What your inventory parts are worth, totalled by the Inventory Asset account they post to, under three costing methods side by side. It is the BI version of the standard Asset Valuation by Account report.

  • LIFO/FIFO Cost: the open cost layers.
  • Std. Cost: standard cost × quantity.
  • Average Cost: the part's total average cost.
Valuation date

Today (the default) values at today's costs and quantities.

An earlier date uses Fishbowl's part cost snapshot, normally taken nightly at 1 am, last taken on or before that day. A part missing from that snapshot had none on hand, so it shows zero, greyed.

Which account

The Inventory Asset account set on the part. A part with none is listed under Use Default, which posts to your default inventory asset account (named on its row).

Hide zero quantity hides parts with nothing on hand today, whatever the date, as the standard report does.

Ties to

The Inventory Valuation Summary values the same stock by location. With today's date and Hide inactive parts off, this report's Average Cost total matches its grand total (Average Cost, every location group), to the cent.

Using it
  • Click an account's row to show or hide its parts; Expand / Collapse do every account.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts within each account (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 parts with a Load all button; totals then cover the parts shown.
