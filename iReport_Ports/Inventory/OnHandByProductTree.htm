📖 How this report works

What it shows

Inventory on hand for each product, organised by its product tree category, by location and tracking. It is the BI version of the standard On Hand by Product Tree report and reads the same data the same way.

A product in two categories is listed under both; one in none is under {Not Assigned}. Quantities are in the part's own unit.

Filters
  • Categories: a category brings everything under it. Pick none for every product.
  • Tracking contains searches the Tracking column (e.g. a lot number or UseBy: 2026-11) and hides untracked lines.
  • Show parts with zero quantity adds parts with no inventory at all, listed as Out of Stock.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a category's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every category.
  • Click a product number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each category (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); Tracking doesn't sort. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Qty, or count lines, by any two of category, product, location group, location, UOM and ABC. A product in two categories counts once unless Category is in the pivot. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 lines with a Load all button.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
