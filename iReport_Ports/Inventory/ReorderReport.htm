📖 How this report works

What it shows

Inventory parts that have fallen to or below their reorder point, and how many to order to get back to the order up to level. It is the BI version of the standard Reorder Report and reads the same data the same way.

Quantities are in each part's own unit of measure, so there are no column totals.

The figures
  • Available = On Hand − Allocated − Not Available + Drop Ship (never below 0).
  • Needed = Order Up To − (On Hand + On Order − Allocated − Not Available), when that is at or below the Reorder Lvl. Otherwise 0.
  • Reordering company wide (Fishbowl's default): one row per part, from the totals of every location group and the part's company-wide levels.
  • Reordering by location group: each group's own stock and levels, under the group's name.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The two "Which parts" options and the columns don't re-run the query.
  • Show all items lists every part, not only those needed.
  • With Include parts with no ROP/OUL off, parts without reorder levels are left out.
  • Click a part or vendor to open it in Fishbowl. Click a location group's or vendor's row to show or hide its parts.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts (within each location group or vendor; again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The view selector next to Filters: By vendor lists each default vendor's parts under its name, ready to raise one purchase order per vendor. Pivot counts the listed parts by any two of vendor, location group, ABC code, part and UOM, or totals a quantity (each part is in its own unit, so total a quantity per part). Click a figure to see the parts behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • A very large catalogue checks the first 10,000 parts by part number, with a Load all button to check them all.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
