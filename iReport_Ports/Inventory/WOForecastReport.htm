📖 How this report works

What it shows

For each open work order, every inventory part on it: the stock on hand now, then every open order that will move that part in the work order's location group, in scheduled date order, with a Running Total of where stock will stand after each one. The work order's own lines are in bold. It is the BI version of the standard WO Forecast Report and reads the same data the same way.

A running total below zero is shown in red: that order would leave you short.

What counts
  • SO: the quantity still to ship goes out; a credit return comes in.
  • PO: the quantity still to receive comes in; a vendor return goes out.
  • WO: finished goods come in and raw goods go out, at each work order's full planned quantity.
  • TO: stock leaves the sending group and arrives at the receiving one.

Quantities are in the part's stocking unit. Qty On Hand is Fishbowl's On Hand for the part in the work order's location group; when some of it is in locations not counted as available (a hold or receiving bin, say), the part's row says how much.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. WO number takes % as a wildcard (20318%).
  • Click a part's row to show or hide its orders; click a work order to hide all its parts. Collapse leaves one row per part: on hand, net change and where it ends up.
  • Click an order or part number to open it in Fishbowl.
  • A part's orders always stay in date order: the running total depends on it. Clicking Qty Change, Running Total or Scheduled Fulfillment Date sorts the parts within each work order by their net change, where they end up, or the date they first run short (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • The pages start each work order on a new page, as the standard report does.
  • A very large result shows its first work orders, up to 10,000 order lines, with a Load all button.
