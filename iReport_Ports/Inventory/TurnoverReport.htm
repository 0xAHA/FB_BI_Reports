📖 How this report works

What it shows

How many times each part's inventory turned over in the date range, per location group. It is the BI version of the standard Turnover Report and reads the same data the same way.

A part is listed for a location group when it had an inventory event there in the range (a receipt, shipment, consumption, adjustment, move…). Active Inventory parts only.

The figures
  • Units Sold: quantity fulfilled on sales order lines whose last fulfilment is in the range, in the part's unit, counted in the sales order's location group.
  • Avg QOH: the average of the on-hand quantity recorded with each inventory event in the range. It is not weighted by time.
  • TO Rate: Units Sold ÷ Avg QOH.
  • Avg. TO Days: the range's length in days ÷ TO Rate. The length counts from the first day to the last, so a 31-day month counts as 30, as in the standard report.
Totals

A group's row and the Company Totals add up Units Sold and Avg QOH. Their TO Rate is the sum of the rows' rates, and their Avg. TO Days divides the range's length by that sum, exactly as the standard report does.

  • Filter changes apply straight away.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its rows; Collapse leaves just the totals.
  • Click a column heading to sort the rows within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order): TO Rate puts the slowest movers first. The totals don't change. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: any figure by part and location group, side by side. Summed, the TO Rates give the same totals as the report. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • Ranking (same selector) lists every row of the table in one list, fastest turner first: # is the rank by TO Rate; equal rates put more Units Sold first, and rows equal on both share a rank. Click a heading to sort by another column; the # stays the TO Rate rank. Its footer is the report's own Company Totals.
  • Company-wide ranking (Filters, with Ranking on) ranks each part across its location groups instead, with TO Rate = its units sold added up ÷ its Avg QOH added up: the turnover of the part's whole stock. That is not the report's own total, which adds the location groups' TO Rates. A part that sold 100 from an average 10 in one group and nothing from 1,000 in another has a report total of 10 + 0 = 10, but turns over 100 ÷ 1,010 = 0.099 company-wide: added-up rates let a small, busy stock hide a large, idle one. Its footer applies the same rule to every part together.
  • Monthly trend runs the report's own query once for each calendar month of the date range (the first and last months cut to the range) and lays the months side by side: TO Rate, or Units Sold (Filters → Figure), for each part or each part in each location group (Filters → Rows). A month is exactly what the Table view shows for that month on its own; a part's figure is its location groups added up, as the report's part totals are. — means no inventory event that month, so the report wouldn't list it. Whole range is the Table view's own figure: monthly TO Rates don't add up to it, because Avg QOH is an average of the month's events. The footer adds each column up, as the report's Company Totals do.
  • The trend loads three months at a time, with progress in the middle of the page; it covers at most 24 months (the last 24 of a longer range) and leaves out months that start after today. A month takes about half a second on a typical database, so 24 months take around 5–15 seconds. Changing a filter while it loads cancels it and starts again; changing the Rows or Figure doesn't reload anything. TO Rates show two decimal places here (Excel has them in full). On paper, a trend of more than six months leaves the description out so the months fit; choose Landscape for a long one.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so rows (whole groups) with a Load all button; the Company Totals then cover the rows shown.
