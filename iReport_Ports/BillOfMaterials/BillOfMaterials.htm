📖 How this report works

What it shows

One bill of materials as the standard Bill of Materials report prints it: each raw good with its quantity and unit, what is available and short, and its unit and total cost. A staged sub-assembly shows what one of it costs to build, with its own BOM's lines under it.

Cost is what one build of the BOM costs: the raw goods' total costs added up. Only the default option of an option group is listed and costed.

Available = on hand − allocated − not available + drop ship in your location groups, in the line's unit, never below 0. Short = quantity − available. Both show for inventory parts only.

Choosing the bill of materials
  • Open Filters and type a BOM number, its description or its finished-good part number. The Active / Inactive chips narrow the list; the arrows step to the previous / next BOM.
  • The report opens on the BOM you looked at last (else the newest active one). When the BI report's Bill_of_Materials setting names a BOM, it opens on that one first.
  • Costing method picks Average, Standard, FIFO or LIFO cost for each part (a part that isn't an inventory part always costs its standard cost).
Using it
  • Click the BOM number to open it in the Bill of Materials module, a part number to open the part.
  • Click a staged item's arrow to fold its sub-BOM; Expand all / Collapse all do every one. Folded stages print as their row.
  • A no conversion tag means the line's unit has no conversion to its part's unit: like the standard report, its cost and availability are taken 1:1.
  • Click a heading to sort (a sub-BOM's lines sort among themselves); drag a heading to move it, its edge to resize. The printed pages keep the BOM order.
  • The App / PDF buttons switch between this view and the printable pages. Print prints the pages; Excel saves the lines.
