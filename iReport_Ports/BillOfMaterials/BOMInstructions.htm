📖 How this report works

What it shows

One bill of materials' instruction steps, as the standard BOM Instructions report prints them: each step's order, name, description, details and link.

Choosing the bill of materials
  • Open Filters and type a BOM number, its description or its finished-good part number: the list shows up to 20 matching BOMs. The Active / Inactive chips narrow it. The arrows step to the previous / next one.
  • The report opens on the BOM you looked at last (else the newest active one). When the BI report's Bill_of_Materials setting names a BOM, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened for a BOM from another app, the report shows that BOM and the picker is hidden.
Using it
  • Click the BOM number to open it in the Bill of Materials module; click a step's URL to open its link.
  • Click the Order or Name heading to sort the steps (again to reverse, a third time for step order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The printed pages keep the step order.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the steps.
