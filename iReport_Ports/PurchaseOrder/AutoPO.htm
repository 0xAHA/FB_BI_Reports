📖 How this report works

What it shows

The parts that need buying, and how many, under the vendor to buy them from. It is the BI version of the standard Auto PO report and asks Fishbowl's own Auto PO engine (the one behind the Auto PO wizard) exactly what the standard report asks it.

Quantities are in each part's own unit of measure, so there are no column totals.

The figures
  • On Hand: stock in locations counted as available.
  • On Order / Allocated: open purchase, sales, manufacture and transfer order lines (scheduled in the date range, when one is set).
  • Available = On Hand − Allocated + Drop Ship (never below 0).
  • A part is listed when On Hand + On Order − Allocated is at or below its Reorder Pnt.
  • Needed = Allocated − On Hand − On Order + Order Up To, raised to the vendor's minimum when Fishbowl is set to use it, in the vendor's unit (Vendor UOM), rounded up for count units.
  • One row per part, under the first of its vendors by name. Only show the default vendor's parts puts each part under its default vendor (or No Vendor); an italic Needed was worked out for the default vendor's part from the engine's figures.
Using it
  • The engine can take a while, and the page waits while it runs. So filters don't apply as you change them: click Run (in the filters or the header). It runs once per location group picked; none = company wide (all your groups together).
  • Click a part or vendor to open it in Fishbowl. Click a vendor's (or location group's) row to show or hide its parts.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts within each vendor (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • Pivot counts the listed parts by any two of vendor, location group, part, UOM and vendor UOM, or totals a quantity (each part is in its own unit, so total per part). Click a figure to see the parts behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
