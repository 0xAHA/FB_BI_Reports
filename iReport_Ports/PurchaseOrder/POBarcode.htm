📖 How this report works

What it shows

Purchase orders of one status, each with its number as a barcode, its vendor, and every line with its part number as a barcode: a sheet to scan when the goods arrive. It is the BI version of the standard Purchase Order Barcode report.

The barcodes

Code 128. The PO barcode carries the PO number, with a P in front when the system property Barcodes have DI is on (Fishbowl's scanners then know it is a PO). Each line's barcode carries its part number in capitals.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Status is one PO status, as in the standard report; PO number matches exactly unless you use % as a wildcard.
  • Click a PO row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every PO. A collapsed PO prints as its PO row only.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each PO (again to reverse, a third time for the PO's own order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole POs) with a Load all button.
