📖 How this report works

What it shows

One purchase order as the document you send the vendor: the BI version of the standard Purchase Order Report, with the same data, layout and options.

  • The title is Purchase Order, or Bid Request (Request for Quote in Australia) while the PO is not issued yet.
  • The vendor box shows the vendor (or remit-to) name and address, the PO's phone and email and the vendor's fax; the ship-to box where the goods go.
  • The lines show the vendor's part number. With multi-currency on, costs and totals are in the PO's currency (USD Unit Cost, USD Total…); the home-currency columns and totals can be switched on.
  • In Canada and Australia (and other non-US companies) the tax columns and the Sales Tax / GST line are available.
Choosing the purchase order
  • Open Filters and type a PO number, a vendor or the vendor's SO number: the list shows up to 20 matching POs in your location groups with their vendor, status, date and total. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next PO.
  • The report opens on the PO you looked at last (else the newest open PO). When the BI report's Purchase_Order setting names a PO, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a purchase order (the Purchase Order module's Apps menu), the report shows that PO and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (header, vendor and ship-to, sections, order fields, item columns, totals) and Settings (titles, custom fields); changes apply straight away. The standard report's saved options are the starting point.
  • Settings → Titles renames any label on the document (leave a box empty for the standard text).
  • Settings → PO custom fields adds up to six PO custom fields as a strip above the lines; Item custom fields adds up to three columns.
  • Click the PO number, the vendor or a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines (again to reverse, a third time for line order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the lines with the columns shown and the totals.
