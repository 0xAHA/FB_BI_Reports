📖 How this report works

What it shows

One shipment as its packing slip: the BI version of the standard Packing Slip (Ship / Packing List), reading the same data the same way. Sales order, purchase order (vendor return / outsourced) and transfer order shipments each get their own layout.

  • The App view is the shipment on screen: the order, ship from / ship to / bill to, dates, carrier and terms, the lines with their tracking and carton, the carton list and the totals.
  • The PDF view is the printed packing slip, page by page, as the standard report prints it.
Choosing the shipment
  • Open Filters and type a shipment number, an SO / PO / TO number or a ship-to name: the list shows the matching shipments with their status, created date and order. The status and Created chips narrow it; with the box empty (or the ▾ button) it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next shipment within those chips.
  • The report opens on the shipment you looked at last (else the newest). When the BI report's Shipment setting names a shipment, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a shipment (the Shipping module's Apps menu), the report shows that shipment and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (header, addresses, sections, items) and Settings (the item columns and the SO custom field strip); changes apply straight away. The standard report's saved options are the starting point (marked stock); Reset options goes back to them.
  • Click a shipment, order, product or part number to open it in Fishbowl; a carton's tracking number opens the carrier's tracking page (UPS, FedEx, USPS).
  • Click a line heading to sort the lines (again to reverse, a third time for line order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The printed document always keeps the standard columns in line order.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this view and the printable pages. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the lines with their quantities, tracking and cartons.
