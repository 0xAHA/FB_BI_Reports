📖 How this report works

What it shows

One shipment's bill of lading, as the standard Bill Of Lading report prints it: where it ships from (the company address of the shipment's location group) and to, the carrier and its SCAC, the bill of lading number and barcode, the order with its packages and freight weight, every item shipped with its quantity and weight, and the terms and signature boxes at the foot of the last page.

Choosing the shipment
  • Open Filters and type a shipment number, an SO / PO / TO number or a ship-to name: the list shows the matching shipments. The status and Created chips narrow it; the arrows step to the previous / next one.
  • The report opens on the shipment you looked at last (else the newest). When the BI report's Shipment setting names a shipment, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened for a shipment from another module (a launch), that shipment is shown and the picker is hidden.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters: trailer and seal numbers, PRO number, NMFC # and class, hazardous materials, the third party to bill freight to, the barcodes, the country and the grand totals. They start from the standard report's saved options and apply straight away.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the lines.
