📖 How this report works

What it shows

The work orders in the date range, by location group or by calendar category: each WO's number, status, category and the days it is scheduled for, was started and was finished. It is the BI version of the standard Work Order Summary report and reads the same data the same way.

Dates, items and users

Date picks which date the range applies to: the fulfillment (scheduled) date, the date created, started, completed or last changed. A work order without that date is left out (an Entered WO has no start date).

A work order is listed when at least one of its items matches the Part number and Item types. Tick Show WO items to list those items under each WO.

Assigned users lists the users assigned to work orders; a WO is listed when any chosen user is assigned to it. (Unassigned) picks WOs with nobody assigned.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Both end days of the range count in full. Choosing no location groups, categories, users, classes or item types means all of them; untick a status to leave its WOs out.
  • Group by Category groups the WOs by calendar category, with the category's colour (Show category colour); WOs without one are under No Category.
  • Columns switch the standard columns on and off; More columns adds fields the standard report doesn't print: the assigned users, the dates created and last changed, the WO's cost (set when it is fulfilled) and its note.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its WOs; Expand / Collapse do every group. Click a WO or a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the WOs within each group, or with WO items the items within each WO (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the number of WOs, the qty to build or the cost by any two of location group, status, category, assigned users, class and the scheduled, created, started or finished date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on WOs by week scheduled and status. A WO with several users counts once, under all its users together. Click a figure to see the WOs behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so WO items (whole WOs) with a Load all button; the counts then cover the WOs shown.
