📖 How this report works

What it shows

What work orders cost, one by one: each work order's finished goods and the parts it uses, with unit and total costs, and the work order's total. It is the BI version of the standard Work Order Cost report and reads the same data the same way.

Projected or actual
  • Open work orders (Entered, Started) are priced by the database's costing method (shown on the status line): average, standard, FIFO or LIFO cost per part, times the quantity needed. The unit cost is per the part's own UOM (the small "/kg" after it); the total converts the line's quantity into that UOM.
  • Fulfilled work orders show what was actually used and what it actually cost.
  • On Hand is the stock in every location group, in the part's UOM.
  • A line whose UOM can't be converted to the part's says not costed and is left out of the totals.
  • The work order's total adds up its raw goods; the finished goods' cost is what they came to.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The standard default is Fulfilled work orders scheduled this month; untick every status for all of them, and pick All dates for every date. A work order without the date you filter on (e.g. Date Finished on an open one) is never listed.
  • Opened from the Work Order module it shows that work order whatever the filters say, and can't be switched.
  • Show details off leaves only the finished goods and the totals.
  • Click a work order's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do them all. The row carries the work order's total; the footer adds them all up.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each item type (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order): Total Cost puts the dearest first. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total cost, the quantity built, the cost per unit built or the number of work orders by any two of finished part, part, item type, work order, manufacture order, status, location group, class and the three dates (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on finished parts by month. Cost per unit built only makes sense by work order fields (not by part or item type). Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole work orders) with a Load all button; the footer total then covers the work orders shown.
  • Work order, manufacture order and part numbers open their Fishbowl module.
