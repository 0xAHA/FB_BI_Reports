📖 How this report works

What it shows

The inventory that open work orders are still waiting to consume: every raw good (and repair raw good) line of an inventory part, with what it would cost at today's prices. It is the BI version of the standard Inv Allocated to Work Orders report and reads the same data the same way.

Qty and cost

Qty is what the line still has allocated: its planned quantity less what has been used, in the line's UOM. A fulfilled work order (or one whose MO is finished) has nothing left, so it shows 0. Unit Cost is today's cost by the costing method you pick (average, standard, or the oldest / newest open FIFO / LIFO cost layer), for one of the line's UOM; Total Cost is Unit Cost × Qty. The company's own method is marked in the list. A line whose UOM can't be converted to the part's is shown not costed.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. WO and part numbers take % as a wildcard; blank lists every one.
  • The date range is on the work order's scheduled date. The standard default, This month, shows only work orders scheduled this month: pick All dates for every open work order.
  • Click a group's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group. A group row carries its totals; a work order's row also shows its status, scheduled date, location group and MO.
  • More columns adds the planned and used quantities (and, grouped by part, each work order's status, scheduled date, location group and MO).
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Total Cost, Qty (in each part's UOM), the number of lines, work orders or parts by any two of part, work order, status, location group, MO, UOM and scheduled date. Part × status shows how much stock each status holds. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Opened from the Work Order module, the report shows that work order, whatever the dates and switches.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole groups) with a Load all button; the group and total rows then cover the lines shown.
