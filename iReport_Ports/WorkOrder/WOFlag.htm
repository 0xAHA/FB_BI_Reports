📖 How this report works

What it shows

The open work orders (Entered or Started) scheduled in the date range, each with every item it needs, and a red Short flag on the raw goods there isn't enough stock of. It is the BI version of the standard Work Order Flag report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Qty Needed is what the work order still needs of the item, in the part's UOM.
  • On Hand is all of the part's stock in the location groups chosen (every location group you can see when none is chosen), whatever is committed or allocated. It shows for inventory parts other than the finished good.
  • Short, in red, is how much more the work order needs than is on hand. Each work order is compared with the whole on hand on its own.
  • Show purchase orders lists each part's open purchase orders and what is still to come on each, in the PO line's UOM.
  • The Total Shortage Summary under the list adds up the raw goods of all the work orders by part, and lists the parts there isn't enough of: with what is on hand, allocated (to any order) and on order.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each work order is scheduled; both end days count in full. Choosing no location groups, categories or users means all of them; "(No category)" and "(No one assigned)" pick the work orders without one.
  • Only work orders with a shortage hides the work orders with nothing short; the summary still covers them all.
  • Sort by orders the work orders by number, date scheduled or category. Show category (and its colour) and MO, status and assigned users add those to each work order's row.
  • Title and Note change what prints at the top of the pages.
  • Click a work order's row to show or hide its items; Expand / Collapse do every work order. Click a work order, MO, part or PO to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the items within each type (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); the summary's headings sort its parts. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Qty Needed, On Hand, Short, the number of lines or of work orders by any two of item type, part, UOM, work order, MO, status, location group, category, assigned users and date scheduled (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on Qty Needed by item type and part, by week. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole work orders) with a Load all button; the summary always covers every work order that matches.
