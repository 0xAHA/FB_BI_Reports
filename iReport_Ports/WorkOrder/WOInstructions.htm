📖 How this report works

What it shows

One work order's instruction steps, as the standard Work Order Instructions report prints them: the scheduled start and finish, the priority, customer job, class, location, status and category, the assigned users (option), and each step with its instruction, the quantity ready and completed.

Printed, the Completed and Status columns are blank for the operator to fill in, unless Quantity completed / Done icon are on. On screen they show only with those options.

Choosing the work order
  • Open Filters and type a WO number, an MO number or a finished-good part number: the list shows up to 20 matching work orders with their finished good, MO, status and date. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next one.
  • The list covers the work orders of your location groups; type an exact number and press Enter to open any other.
  • The report opens on the work order you looked at last (else the newest open one). When the BI report's Work_Order setting names a work order, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a work order (the Work Order module's Apps menu), the report shows that work order and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (barcode, times, assigned users, step details, quantity completed, done icon and the columns), with the standard report's saved options as the starting point. Changes apply straight away.
  • Click the work order, its MO or a step's Link to open it.
  • Click the Step, Ready, Completed or Status heading to sort the steps (again to reverse, a third time for step order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The printed pages keep the step order.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the steps with their details, quantities and URLs.
