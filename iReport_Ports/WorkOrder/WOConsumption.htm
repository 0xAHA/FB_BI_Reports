📖 How this report works

What it shows

What fulfilled work orders planned to use against what they actually consumed: every component (raw good) of every work order finished in the date range. It is the BI version of the standard Work Order Consumption report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Qty is what the work order planned to use of the part; Consumed what it used. Both are in the part's own UOM, converted from the line's.
  • Difference is Qty − Consumed: + (green) used less than planned, − (red) used more. Blank means exactly as planned.
  • A line whose UOM has no conversion to the part's (⚠) is shown in its own UOM and left out of the part's totals; the ⚠ on a total says which lines it leaves out.
  • A group whose lines are in different units (a work order's kg, ea and L, say) shows mixed instead of totals: adding them together would mean nothing. Its row still counts the lines over and under plan.
  • Labour and other lines Fishbowl doesn't pick are set to their planned quantity when the work order is finished, so they always match.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each work order was finished; both end days count in full. Opened from the Work Order module it shows that work order whatever the filters, and can't be switched.
  • Group by Part # or WO #. Click a group's row to show or hide its lines; Expand / Collapse do every group. Show group totals puts each group's totals on its row.
  • Summary: Include Summary (the default) adds a table with one row per part (or work order) under the list; Summary Only shows that table alone; No Summary leaves it out. Its Over / Under columns count the lines that used more / less than planned.
  • Columns switches the standard report's columns on and off, in the list and the summary.
  • Click a column heading to sort the lines within each group (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order); the summary's headings sort its rows. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the Difference, Qty, Consumed (in each part's UOM), Consumed % of Qty, work orders or lines over / under plan by any two of part, WO, MO, location group, item type and date finished (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on each part's difference by month. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole groups) with a Load all button; the summary has its own query and covers every match up to 10,000 rows.
