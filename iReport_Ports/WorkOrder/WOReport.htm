📖 How this report works

What it shows

One work order, as the standard Work Order Report prints it: its BOM and BOM quantity, the scheduled and finished dates, class and priority, the note, the sales order line it builds for, and every item with its type, part, description, quantities and the stock available.

Available is the part's on hand, less allocated and not available, plus drop ship, over every location group (0 when that is negative). Item Qty is the item's quantity for one unit of the work order.

The company address in the header is the address of the work order's location group. The number is printed with a "W" in front when the company's barcodes carry the order type.

Choosing the work order
  • Open Filters and type a WO number, an MO number or a finished-good part number: the list shows up to 20 matching work orders with their finished good, MO, status and date. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next one.
  • The list covers the work orders of your location groups; type an exact number and press Enter to open any other.
  • The report opens on the work order you looked at last (else the newest open one). When the BI report's Work_Order setting names a work order, it opens on that one first.
  • Opened from a work order (the Work Order module's Apps menu), the report shows that work order and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Every option of the standard report is in Filters (barcode, sales order details and the item columns), with the standard report's saved options as the starting point. Changes apply straight away.
  • Tracking shows the lot, serial and other values recorded on a fulfilled work order's items (blank while it is open, as in the standard report).
  • Click the work order, MO, BOM, sales order or a part to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click an item heading to sort the items (again to reverse, a third time for the report's order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it. The printed pages follow the order on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the items with every quantity and UOM.
