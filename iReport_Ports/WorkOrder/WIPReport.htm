📖 How this report works

What it shows

The value of the parts sitting in manufacturing for open work orders: the quantity of each part in your Manufacturing type locations and what it is worth at today's cost. It is the BI version of the standard WIP Report and reads the same data the same way.

Which parts

A part is listed when it has stock in a Manufacturing location and is on an open work order (Entered or Started) whose pick for it is Finished. Its quantity is all of its stock in Manufacturing locations, in the part's own UOM; Asset Value is that quantity times the unit cost.

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. Costing method: Average or Standard cost as the standard report, or the FIFO / LIFO cost (the oldest / newest open cost layer). The company's own method is marked.
  • Show work orders lists, under each part, the open work orders it was picked for: WO #, scheduled date and the quantity picked, in the work order's UOM. Show locations splits the part's quantity and value by manufacturing location.
  • With either on, click a part's row to show or hide what is under it; Expand / Collapse do every part.
  • Click a column heading to sort the parts (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order): click Asset Value twice for the biggest first. A part's work orders and locations follow the same heading where it means something. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: total Asset Value, Qty or the number of tags by any two of location group, location, part and UOM. Part × Location shows where the WIP value sits. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so part / location rows (whole parts) with a Load all button; the Grand Total then covers the parts shown.
