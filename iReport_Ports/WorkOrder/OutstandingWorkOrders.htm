📖 How this report works

What it shows

Every open work order (Entered or Started) due in the date range: the part it makes, the sales order and customer it is for (when its manufacture order came from a sales order), the day it was started, the day it is due and how many days it is past due. It is the BI version of the standard Outstanding Work Orders report and reads the same data the same way.

The rows

One row per finished good the work order makes (a repair WO shows the item it repairs), so a WO that makes two things has two rows. Past Due counts calendar days from the due date to today: "3 days past due", "Due today", "Due in 5 days".

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each WO is due; both end days count in full. Choosing no location groups means all of yours.
  • Sort by orders the list as the standard report does. Sorted by location group, each group has its own heading row: click it to show or hide its WOs; Expand / Collapse do every group.
  • More columns adds the WO's status, assigned users, calendar category (with its colour), its manufacture order and the qty it makes.
  • Click a WO, part, sales order, customer or MO to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the WOs (within each location group when sorted by location group; again to reverse, a third time for the Sort by order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: the number of WOs, rows, the qty or the days past due by any two of location group, customer, SO, part, status, assigned users, category, due band (how far past due) and the due or started date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on WOs by due band and location group. Click a figure to see the rows behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so rows (whole WOs) with a Load all button; the counts then cover the WOs shown.
