📖 How this report works

What it shows

How much each fulfilled work order actually made against what it was ordered to make: every finished part of every work order finished in the date range. It is the BI version of the standard Production Yield report and reads the same data the same way.

The figures
  • Order Qty is the work order's target for the part; Actual Yield what it made, both in the line's UOM.
  • Variance is Actual Yield ÷ Order Qty: 100% made exactly what was ordered; under 100% (red) made less, over (green) made more.
  • A work order that made several finished parts has a row for each.
  • Grouped by part, the part's row adds its lines up in the part's own UOM and gives its yield: everything made ÷ everything ordered. A line whose UOM has no conversion to the part's is left out of those totals and flagged.
Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each work order was finished; both end days count in full. Opened from the Work Order module it shows that work order whatever the dates, and can't be switched.
  • Group by part adds a row per part with its totals and yield. Click a part's row to show or hide its work orders; Expand / Collapse do every part.
  • Click a column heading to sort (within each part when grouped; again to reverse, a third time for the standard order): Variance puts the worst yields first. Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: Yield %, the quantities ordered and made (in each part's UOM), work orders, lines or lines under / over target by any two of part, location group, manufacture order and date finished (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on each part's yield by month. Click a figure to see the lines behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so lines (whole parts) with a Load all button; the footer counts then cover the lines shown.
