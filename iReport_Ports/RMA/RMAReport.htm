📖 How this report works

What it shows

The return ticket for one RMA: the products the customer is sending back, with the quantity, the issue, the type of return (credit, replacement, substitution, repair) and its resolution, and under each item its tracking and note. It is the BI version of the standard RMA Report: same data, same layout.

The header shows the customer, when the RMA was created and expires, its status and location group, the company address for that group and the RMA number with its barcode.

Choosing the RMA
  • Open Filters and type an RMA number or a customer: the list shows up to 20 matching RMAs with their status and date. The status chips narrow it; with the box empty it lists the newest. The arrows step to the previous / next RMA.
  • The report opens on the RMA you looked at last (else the newest open RMA in your location groups). The BI report has no setting that names an RMA: Fishbowl's report settings have no RMA picker.
  • Opened from an RMA (the RMA module's Apps menu), the report shows that RMA and the picker is hidden, so it can't be changed by accident.
Using it
  • Barcode (Filters) prints the RMA number as a barcode, as the standard report does by default.
  • More columns (Filters) adds fields the standard report doesn't print: the substitute product, the date resolved, the vendor and vendor RMA, and the ship / return / cross-ship flags. On the printed pages they print as one more line under the item.
  • Click the RMA number, the customer, a product or a vendor to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click an item heading to sort the items (again to reverse, a third time for line order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • Excel saves the RMA's items with their tracking and notes.
