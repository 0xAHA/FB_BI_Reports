📖 How this report works

What it shows

The RMAs (return merchandise authorizations) issued in the date range, by location group: each RMA's number, customer, the day it was issued, the day it expires and its status. It is the BI version of the standard RMA Summary report and reads the same data the same way.

RMA details

Tick Show RMA details to list each RMA's items under it: the product coming back, its quantity and unit, the issue, the type (credit, replacement, substitution, repair), the resolution and the note.

An RMA is listed when at least one of its items matches the filters (item type, product, issue).

Using it
  • Filter changes apply straight away. The dates are the day each RMA was issued; both end days count in full. Choosing no location groups, customers, types or issues means all of them; untick a status to leave its RMAs out.
  • Order by customer lists each location group's RMAs by customer name, then number.
  • More columns (with RMA details) adds fields the standard report doesn't print: the product description, the substitute product, the date resolved, the vendor and vendor RMA, and the ship / return / cross-ship flags.
  • Click a location group's row to show or hide its RMAs; Expand / Collapse do every group. Click an RMA, customer, product or vendor to open it in Fishbowl.
  • Click a column heading to sort the RMAs within each location group, or with RMA details the items within each RMA (again to reverse, a third time for the standard order). Drag a heading onto another to move the column; drag a heading's right edge to resize it. Your layout is remembered; Settings → Reset column layout undoes it.
  • The Table / Pivot selector switches to a pivot: returned Qty, the number of items or the number of RMAs by any two of location group, customer, status, product, UOM, type, issue, resolution, vendor and the issued, expires or resolved date (by day, week, month, quarter or year). It opens on Qty by product and type. Click a figure to see the items behind it. Print, PDF and Excel follow the view on screen.
  • The App / PDF buttons in the header switch between this interactive view and the printable pages, laid out like the standard report. In PDF view pick the paper size and orientation in the bar above the pages; they are remembered. Print prints those pages.
  • A very large result shows its first 10,000 or so items (whole RMAs) with a Load all button; the counts then cover the RMAs shown.
